## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Push Gas Lawn Mower Under 300

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before making a purchase, assess the size and terrain of your yard. If you have a flat, quarter-acre lot, a standard manual push mower is ideal. However, if your yard has significant slopes or you have physical limitations, a self-propelled model like the Craftsman M215 will save you a lot of effort. Also, consider whether you prefer to bag your clippings for a clean look or mulch them back into the soil for added nutrients.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($150-$300 ranges)
While our focus is under 300, prices can vary between 180 and 299 depending on the engine size and features. The 180 to 220 range typically offers basic push mowers with 2-in-1 functionality. Moving into the 250 to 300 range usually gets you larger engines, 3-in-1 versatility, high wheels for better traction, or even entry-level self-propulsion.

### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications

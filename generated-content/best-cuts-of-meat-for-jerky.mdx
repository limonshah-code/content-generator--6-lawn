## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Cuts Of Meat For Jerky

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you choose a jerky product or a cut of meat, consider how you intend to use it. If you are looking for a quick snack during a commute, individually wrapped sticks are superior. However, if you are planning a long hiking trip, a bulk bag of dry-style beef jerky might be more efficient as it is lighter and provides more protein per ounce. For those who want to control every ingredient, investing in a slicer kit is the best path forward.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations

## What Is the Best Way to Choose cuts of meat for jerky?

Choosing the right cut of meat is the most important step in the jerky-making process. Here is a step-by-step guide to making the best selection:

1.  **Select Lean Cuts:** Fat does not dry out; it turns rancid. Always choose the leanest cuts available. Eye of round, top round, and bottom round are the gold standards.
2.  **Look for Flank Steak:** If you want a more tender, premium jerky with a distinct grain, flank steak is an excellent, albeit more expensive, choice.
3.  **Check the Grain:** For a traditional chewy jerky, slice against the grain. If you prefer a very tough jerky that takes time to eat, slice with the grain.
4.  **Avoid Marbling:** While marbling is great for a grilled steak, it is the enemy of jerky. Trim any visible fat before you begin the marination process.
5.  **Professional Tip:** Partially freeze your meat for 1-2 hours before slicing. This firms up the muscle fibers and allows your slicer or knife to glide through for perfectly uniform pieces.
6.  **Freshness Matters:** Always start with the freshest meat possible. While jerky is a preservation method, starting with meat near its expiration date will result in an inferior final product.

## FAQ

### What is the best cuts of meat for jerky for beginners?
For beginners, the Eye of Round is widely considered the best cut. it is very lean, relatively inexpensive, and has a uniform shape that makes slicing easy. it is a forgiving cut that works well with almost any marinade and drying method.

### How much should I spend on cuts of meat for jerky?
If you are buying raw meat, look for sales on round roasts which typically go for $5-$8 per pound. If buying finished jerky, $2 per ounce is a fair price for high-quality, clean-label products.

### What features are most important in cuts of meat for jerky?
The most important feature is the fat content. You want the lowest fat possible to ensure a long shelf life. For tools, the ability to create uniform slices is the most critical feature to look for.

### How do I maintain my cuts of meat for jerky?
Unopened commercial jerky should be kept in a cool, dry place. Once opened, most should be consumed within 3-7 days or refrigerated to maintain freshness. Homemade jerky should be stored in airtight containers or vacuum-sealed bags.

### Are expensive cuts of meat for jerky worth it?
Cuts like flank steak are more expensive but offer a much more tender and flavorful experience. If you find standard round jerky too tough, the extra investment in flank steak is definitely worth it.

### What brands make the best cuts of meat for jerky?
Chef’s Cut and People’s Choice are currently leading the market in terms of meat quality and clean ingredients. Jack Link’s remains the best for availability and classic flavors.

### How long do cuts of meat for jerky typically last?
Commercial jerky usually has a shelf life of 12 months if unopened. Homemade jerky lasts about 1-2 months in an airtight container at room temperature or much longer if frozen.

### Can I find good cuts of meat for jerky on a budget?
Yes, buying bulk beef roasts like the Bottom Round when they are on sale is the best way to get high-quality meat on a budget. For pre-made snacks, look for large bags (9oz or 16oz) to get a better price per ounce.

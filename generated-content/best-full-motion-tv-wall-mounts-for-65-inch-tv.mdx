## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Full Motion Tv Wall Mounts For 65 Inch Tv

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you purchase a mount, you must identify your specific viewing needs. Are you mounting in a corner, or do you need the TV to face a secondary seating area like a kitchen? If you need a corner mount, look for models with extra-long extensions (30 inches or more). Furthermore, ensure the mount supports your TV's VESA pattern, which is the standard distance between the four mounting holes on the back of your television. A 65-inch TV typically uses 400x400mm or 600x400mm patterns.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations
You can find reliable full-motion mounts for 65-inch TVs in the $30 to $80 range. Budget options around $30 to $40 usually offer basic articulating arms and standard extensions. If you move into the $50 to $80 range, you start seeing premium features like UL certification, thicker steel, tool-free adjustments, and significantly longer extensions. Investing a little more often provides better post-installation leveling options and smoother movement.

### Key Features - Most important technical specifications
The three most important specs are weight capacity, extension length, and tilt/swivel range. Most 65-inch TVs weigh between 40 and 70 pounds, so a mount with a 99-pound capacity is usually sufficient. Extension length determines how much you can swivel the TV; a 15-inch extension is standard, but if you need to turn the TV 90 degrees, you will need a much longer arm. Tilt is also crucial for mounting the TV above eye level to prevent neck strain and glare.

### Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Look for mounts made of cold-rolled steel or those that mention robot welding technology. These manufacturing processes ensure that the joints are strong and that the arms won't sag over time under the weight of your TV. UL listing is another mark of high quality, as it means the product has been independently tested for safety and durability.

### Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Stick with brands that specialize in mounting solutions, such as Mounting Dream, Perlegear, or USX Mount. ## What Is the Best Way to Choose full motion tv wall mounts for 65 inch tv?

Choosing the right mount requires a systematic approach to ensure safety and functionality. Follow these steps for the best results:

1.  **Check your TV specs:** Locate your TV's weight and VESA pattern in the manual or on the manufacturer's website. Ensure the mount you choose supports these specific numbers with a safety margin.
2.  **Locate your wall studs:** Use a stud finder to determine if your studs are 16 inches apart or more. Most mounts are designed for 16-inch spacing. If yours are wider, you will need a specialized wide-plate mount.
3.  **Determine your required extension:** Measure how much you need the TV to turn. For a 65-inch TV to turn 45 degrees, you typically need an extension of at least 15-17 inches.
4.  **Consider the installation height:** If you are mounting the TV higher than eye level, prioritize a mount with a generous downward tilt (at least 10-15 degrees) to maintain a good viewing angle.
5.  **Verify the hardware:** Ensure the mount comes with a comprehensive hardware kit that includes spacers and various screw sizes, as different TV brands use different mounting depths.
6.  **Avoid Drywall Only:** Never attempt to mount a 65-inch TV to drywall alone. Always ensure the mount is secured into wood studs or solid concrete.

## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Hand Saw For Cutting Trees

Selecting the right hand saw is about matching the tool to the specific vegetation in your yard. Not all saws are created equal, and understanding the nuances can save you from a lot of unnecessary physical labor.

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Consider the thickness of the branches you intend to cut. If you are mostly trimming small shrubs or fruit trees, an 8 to 10-inch folding saw is ideal. However, for mature trees with branches over 6 inches in diameter, you will need a larger, fixed-blade saw like the 18-inch Corona. Additionally, think about where you will be using it; a folding saw is essential for hikers and campers, while a fixed D-handle saw is better for dedicated yard work.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($15-$60 ranges)
Manual hand saws typically range from $15 to $40. Budget options are great for occasional light use, while the $30-$40 range usually gets you better steel quality and more comfortable grips. If you decide to go with a mini cordless chainsaw, expect to spend between $50 and $100 for a quality kit that includes batteries.

### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look at the Teeth Per Inch (TPI). A lower TPI (around 7-8) means the saw has larger teeth, which is better for fast, aggressive cuts through green wood. A higher TPI provides a smoother finish but is slower. Triple-ground or triple-cut teeth are a must-have as they cut on both the push and pull stroke, significantly increasing efficiency.

### 4. Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
High-carbon steel (like SK5) is the gold standard for saw blades because it holds an edge longer. Look for blades that are impulse-hardened, which means the teeth have been heat-treated to be harder than the rest of the blade. Chrome or Teflon coatings are also beneficial as they prevent rust and reduce the friction that causes the blade to stick in wet wood.

### 5. Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Established brands like Corona and Jorgensen have decades of experience in tool manufacturing and often offer better customer support. While newer brands can offer great value, established ones usually have more rigorous quality control and more ergonomic handle designs that have been refined over years of user feedback.

### 6. Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage
If you have limited storage space or need to carry your tool over long distances, a folding saw is the clear winner. Ensure the folding mechanism has a robust lock to prevent accidental closure. For those with large properties, a sheath for a fixed-blade saw is a critical safety accessory to consider.

## Is it A Wise Decision to Buy hand saw for cutting trees?

Investing in a high-quality hand saw is one of the smartest decisions a homeowner or outdoor enthusiast can make. While power tools like full-sized chainsaws offer raw power, they come with significant downsides: they are heavy, noisy, require gas or constant charging, and carry a much higher risk of injury. A hand saw, by contrast, is always ready to work. It requires no fuel, creates no pollution, and allows for much more precise cuts, which is healthier for the tree's recovery.

For most suburban yards, a hand saw can handle 90% of pruning tasks more quickly than it takes to get a gas chainsaw started and geared up. The long-term benefits include a one-time cost with very little maintenance beyond occasional cleaning. Over time, the physical exercise gained from manual sawing is a secondary benefit, and the quiet operation ensures you remain on good terms with your neighbors. When compared to the cost of hiring a professional tree service for minor trimming, a $30 hand saw pays for itself in the first ten minutes of use.

## What Is the Best Way to Choose hand saw for cutting trees?

To choose the best saw, start by measuring the average diameter of the branches you need to cut. A good rule of thumb is that the blade should be at least twice as long as the branch's thickness. Next, evaluate your own physical comfort. If you have arthritis or limited grip strength, a motorized mini chainsaw like the Seesii model is a much better choice than a manual one.

Professional arborists recommend looking at the blade shape. Curved blades are superior for overhead work because they pull the saw into the wood, whereas straight blades are better for controlled, detailed cuts at waist level. Avoid the mistake of buying the cheapest saw available; these often have stamped teeth that cannot be sharpened and will dull after just a few uses. Instead, look for impulse-hardened teeth which are designed to stay sharp for the life of the tool. Finally, always check the locking mechanism on folding saws; a lock that holds the blade in both the open and closed positions is the safest option.

## FAQ

**What is the best hand saw for cutting trees for beginners?**
For beginners, a 10-inch folding saw with a curved blade is the best choice. It offers a good balance of cutting power and safety. The curved blade helps keep the saw in the groove, preventing the blade from jumping out and causing injury. Models like the WEIMELTOY or GROWIT are excellent starting points.

**How much should I spend on hand saw for cutting trees?**
You should expect to spend between $25 and $40 for a high-quality manual saw. While you can find options for under $20, they often use inferior steel that dulls quickly. Investing an extra $10 usually gets you SK5 steel and a much more comfortable, ergonomic handle.

**What features are most important in hand saw for cutting trees?**
The most important features are the steel quality (look for SK5), the tooth design (triple-ground is best), and a comfortable, non-slip grip. If it is a folding saw, a robust locking mechanism is the most critical safety feature to evaluate before purchase.

**How do I maintain my hand saw for cutting trees?**
Maintenance is simple: after each use, wipe the blade with a rag to remove sap and moisture. Occasionally apply a light coat of WD-40 or mineral oil to prevent rust. If the blade becomes gummed up with resin, use a bit of soapy water or a specialized blade cleaner to restore its smoothness.

**Are expensive hand saw for cutting trees worth it?**
Yes, they are. More expensive saws use better heat treatment on the teeth, meaning they will stay sharp for years rather than months. They also feature better ergonomics, which prevents blisters and hand fatigue, making your yard work much more enjoyable and efficient.

**What brands make the best hand saw for cutting trees?**
Corona, Jorgensen, and REXBETI are all highly regarded in the industry. Corona is often preferred by professionals for their heavy-duty curved saws, while REXBETI and GROWIT offer excellent folding designs that are popular with homeowners and campers.

**How long do hand saw for cutting trees typically last?**
A well-maintained hand saw with impulse-hardened teeth can last 5 to 10 years for a typical homeowner. The blade itself will eventually dull, and while some can be sharpened, many modern saws are designed to have the blade replaced once the teeth have lost their edge.

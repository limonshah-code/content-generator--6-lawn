## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Ice Tea Pitcher

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before making a purchase, ask yourself how you plan to use the pitcher. Are you brewing sun tea on the porch, or do you need something that can handle boiling water for a quick steep? If you are a solo drinker, a 2-quart model is perfect, but families or those who host should look at 1-gallon options. Compatibility with your refrigerator is also key—measure your door shelf height before buying a tall 1-gallon model to ensure it fits.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($X-$Y ranges)
You can find a reliable pitcher in several price brackets. Budget plastic models usually range from $10 to $15. Mid-range glass or premium Tritan plastic carafes often fall between $20 and $35. High-end sets or large-capacity brewers with integrated stainless steel infusers can cost $40 or more. Setting a budget early helps you decide between basic utility and premium features like airtight seals or protective shells.

### Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for features that simplify your life. An airtight seal is a game-changer if you have a crowded fridge and need to store the pitcher horizontally. Material choice is the biggest factor in durability. Borosilicate glass is superior to standard glass because it resists thermal shock. Look for thick handles that are part of the main body or securely attached with metal bands, as this is the most common point of failure.

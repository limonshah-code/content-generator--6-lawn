## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Indian Tandoori Oven

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Decide if you are looking for the cooking vessel itself or the flavor components. If you want to bake naan that bubbles and chars, an electric tandoori oven is essential. However, if you already have a high-heat grill, a premium masala or paste might be all you need to achieve the right flavor profile. Consider how often you cook Indian cuisine before investing in dedicated hardware.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($10-$200 ranges)
A high-quality spice blend or paste typically ranges from $8 to $25 depending on the size and organic certifications. A dedicated electric tandoori oven is a larger investment, usually falling between $150 and $300. Setting a budget helps you decide between individual meal packets or bulk jars that offer better value over time.

### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications
For ovens, look for a high wattage (at least 1200W) and a temperature range that exceeds 700 degrees Fahrenheit.

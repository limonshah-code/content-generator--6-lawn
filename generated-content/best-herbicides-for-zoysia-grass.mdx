## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Herbicides For Zoysia Grass

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you buy, identify whether you need a selective or non-selective herbicide. Selective products like Atticus Empero are designed to kill specific weeds without harming your zoysia turf. Non-selective products like glyphosate will kill anything they touch, making them better for driveways or clearing large areas before replanting. Always verify that the label explicitly mentions zoysia grass as a compatible turf type to avoid accidental lawn damage.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($15-$80 ranges)
Herbicides vary greatly in price based on concentration and active ingredients. Budget-friendly options usually range from $15 to $30 and are often non-selective concentrates. Professional-grade selective herbicides like halosulfuron-methyl can cost $40 to $80 for a small bottle but are highly concentrated and target the most difficult weeds. Consider the square footage you need to treat to determine the best value for your situation.

### Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for the concentration of active ingredients and the rainfast time. A product that is rainfast in 2 to 4 hours is superior if you live in a rainy climate. Additionally, check if the product requires a separate surfactant. A surfactant helps the herbicide stick to the weed leaves, which is essential for effectiveness. Some modern products include this in the formula, saving you an extra step and additional cost.

### Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
In the world of herbicides, quality is measured by the stability of the chemical formula and the reliability of the kill. Look for products with a long shelf life, as you likely won't use the entire bottle in one season. Professional brands often offer better packaging that prevents leaking and ensures the active ingredients don't degrade over time when stored in a cool, dry place.

### Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Stick to established brands like Scotts, Petra, and Atticus. These companies invest heavily in research and testing to ensure their products are safe and effective. Established manufacturers also provide detailed labels with precise mixing instructions, which are critical for safety and success. Buying from a reputable brand also ensures you have access to customer support if you have questions about application.

### Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage
Herbicides come in various sizes from small 1.33-ounce powder bottles to 1-gallon liquid concentrates. If you have a small suburban yard, a compact pre-measured packet system like the Q-Pak might be better than storing large jugs of chemicals. ## What Is the Best Way to Choose herbicides for zoysia grass?

The best way to choose the right product is to first identify the specific weeds that are plaguing your lawn. Not all herbicides kill all weeds. For example, nutsedge requires a specific chemical called halosulfuron, whereas crabgrass is better handled with mesotrione. Once you know your target, follow these steps:

1. Identify the weed species using a guide or a plant identification app.
2. Check the herbicide label to ensure it is safe for zoysia grass.
3. Determine if you need a pre-emergent (to prevent seeds) or a post-emergent (to kill existing weeds).
4. Evaluate the area size to choose between a concentrate or a ready-to-use spray.
5. Check if a surfactant is required and buy it if it is not included.

A common mistake is applying the wrong chemical at the wrong time. For example, applying a pre-emergent after the weeds have already sprouted will not yield results. Conversely, applying a non-selective herbicide on your lawn will leave dead brown patches. Always read the label twice and measure your chemicals exactly. If you are unsure, start with a smaller spot test in an inconspicuous area of the yard before treating the entire lawn.

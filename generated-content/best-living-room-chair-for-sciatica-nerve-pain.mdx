## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Living Room Chair For Sciatica Nerve Pain

### Find Your Need
Before making a purchase, identify where your pain is most acute. If you feel pressure directly on your tailbone, a cushion with a U-shaped cutout is essential. If your pain radiates from your lower back, prioritize a chair or pillow with robust lumbar support. Consider how long you sit at one time; longer sessions require higher-density foam or gel to prevent bottoming out.

### Budget
Living room solutions for sciatica range from $25 for basic cushions to $500 for high-end massage recliners. A budget of $50 to $100 can usually get you a high-quality orthopedic set (seat and lumbar support).

## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Kathy Ireland Mattress Topper

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you make a purchase, determine whether you need to soften a too-firm mattress, add support to a sagging one, or simply protect your investment. A 2-inch fiber pad is great for basic comfort, while a 3 or 4-inch memory foam or featherbed is better for significant pressure relief. Check your mattress depth as well; you will need a topper with pockets or straps that match your bed's height.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($50-$200 ranges)
Basic mattress pads and fiberfill toppers typically fall in the $50 to $80 range and provide a nice surface upgrade.

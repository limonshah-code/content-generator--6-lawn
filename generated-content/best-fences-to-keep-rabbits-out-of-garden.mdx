## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Fences To Keep Rabbits Out Of Garden

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before purchasing, assess whether you need to protect a single plant, a raised bed, or your entire yard. If you have soft soil, a no-dig fence with long spikes is excellent. For rocky ground, you might consider individual cages or liquid repellents that do not require deep soil insertion.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($X-$Y ranges)
Basic wire mesh rolls can start as low as $25 and provide extensive coverage if you are willing to do the cutting yourself. High-end decorative metal panels usually range from $40 to $80 for a 10-foot section. Consider how many feet you need to cover to avoid under-budgeting for large perimeters.

### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for grid spacing; rabbits can squeeze through anything larger than 2 inches. Height is also crucial; 13-17 inches is standard for rabbits, while 24 inches is better if you also have deer or larger dogs. Rustproofing, such as vinyl coating or baked-on paint, is non-negotiable for outdoor items.

### 4. Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Check for welded joints rather than just twisted wires. A thick metal gauge will resist bending if a larger animal bumps into it. For liquid repellents, check for rain resistance to ensure the product does not wash away during the first light drizzle.

### 5. Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Reputable brands like ULIOK and Goovilla offer better customer service and higher quality control on their welds and coatings. Established brands often include better accessories like heavy-duty zip ties or specialized cutting tools that cheaper alternatives might skip.

### 6. Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage
If you live in a climate with harsh winters, you may want to store your fences. Look for folding designs or panels that snap apart easily. Modular systems like plant cages are much easier to store in a garage or shed than long, rigid wire rolls.

## What Is the Best Way to Choose fences to keep rabbits out of garden?

Choosing the right fence starts with measuring your perimeter. Once you know the length, follow these steps:

1. **Check the Soil:** If your soil is hard, prioritize fences with sharpened, inclined spikes.
2. **Identify the Pests:** If you only have small rabbits, a 13-inch height is fine. If you have hares or larger dogs, go for 24 inches.
3. **Select the Spacing:** Ensure the gap between wires is 1.5 inches or less to prevent small bunnies from squeezing through.
4. **Decide on Aesthetics:** If your garden is in the front yard, choose decorative arched panels. For a back vegetable patch, functional wire mesh might suffice.
5. **Consider Installation:** If you are working alone, look for cable tie systems which are easier to manage than traditional snaps.

Avoid the common mistake of buying a fence that is too short or has large gaps, as rabbits are surprisingly agile and can compress their bodies to fit through small openings.

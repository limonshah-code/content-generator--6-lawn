## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Cough Suppressant For Dry Cough

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you purchase a cough suppressant, you must identify the nature of your cough. Is it purely dry and tickly, or do you feel mucus in your chest? If it is strictly dry, a pure suppressant with Dextromethorphan is your best bet. If there is congestion, look for a DM formula that includes an expectorant like Guaifenesin. Also consider when you need the relief; nighttime coughs benefit from 12-hour formulas, while daytime coughs might only need 4-8 hour protection.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($X-$Y ranges)
Pricing for cough medicine varies based on the brand and the quantity. You can expect to spend anywhere from $10 to $25. Generic bulk softgels usually offer the best value, often costing under $15 for a 100-count bottle. Name-brand 12-hour liquids are more premium, typically ranging between $15 and $22 per bottle. If you have a large family, bulk purchases are highly recommended to save on the cost per dose.

### Key Features - Most important technical specifications
The most critical feature is the active ingredient. Dextromethorphan HBr is standard for 8-hour relief, while Dextromethorphan Polistirex is used for 12-hour extended release. Pay attention to whether the formula is non-drowsy, which is essential for daytime productivity. Additionally, check if the product is sugar-free or alcohol-free if you have specific dietary needs or sensitivities.

### Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction

Gifts in this category are generally affordable. Small items like greeting cards or plant magnets usually fall under the $10 mark. Middle-range items like kitchen towels, trellises, or mugs typically cost between $15 and $25. Premium DIY kits or high-quality propagation stations can range from $30 to $50. ### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for technical details that impact the health of the plants. For planters, integrated drainage is a non-negotiable feature. For trellises, the material should be rust-resistant iron or plastic. ### 4. Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Durability is key for items that will be around water and soil. Choose high boron silicon glass for vases as it resists cracking. For metal items, powder coating is essential to prevent rust. High-quality resin is preferred for outdoor decor as it withstands UV rays better than cheap plastics. Always check for reinforced stitching in fabric gifts to prevent fraying.

### 5. Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
While many plant gifts come from boutique brands, looking for companies like Rolife or XXXFLOWER ensures you are getting a product with tested instructions and reliable customer service. Established brands often offer satisfaction guarantees and have a track record of positive user feedback regarding the longevity of their products.

### 6. Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage
Always measure the space where the gift might go. A desktop terrarium needs to be compact, while a DIY greenhouse requires a dedicated shelf. If the recipient lives in a small apartment, consider smaller accessories like magnets or kitchen towels rather than large floor trellises. Portability is also a factor if they frequently move their plants around for sunlight.

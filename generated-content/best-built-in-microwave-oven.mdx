## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Built In Microwave Oven

### Find Your Need
Before you buy, you must determine if you need a standard built-in, a drawer style, or a convection combo. Standard built-ins are great for simple reheating, while drawer models are perfect for under-counter installations to save space. If you lack a second wall oven, a convection microwave combo can serve as a powerful secondary baking station.

### Budget
Built-in microwaves typically range from $300 to $1,500. Entry-level units ($300-$500) provide standard heating and basic sensors. Mid-range models ($500-$800) often add inverter technology and better finishes. Premium models ($800+) feature convection baking, air frying, and drawer mechanisms that offer a high-end integrated look.

### Key Features
Look for Inverter Technology which provides constant power rather than pulsing on and off. Smart Sensors are also vital as they measure humidity to prevent dry food. If you enjoy crispy textures, ensure the unit has an Air Fry or Broil mode with a dedicated heating element.

### Quality & Durability
Prioritize stainless steel interiors over painted ones as they are more durable and easier to clean. Check the weight capacity of the hinges if it is a drop-down door. A unit with a high-quality glass turntable and responsive touch controls will generally last much longer in a busy household.

### Brand Reputation
Established brands like GE, Panasonic, and Toshiba have extensive service networks and readily available replacement parts. Choosing a brand with a strong history in microwave technology ensures that the specialized components like magnetrons and inverters are reliable for the long haul.

### Portability/Size
For built-ins, measurements are critical. You must measure your cabinet cutout width, depth, and height precisely. Consider the clearance needed for the door to swing or the drawer to extend. Always check if a trim kit is included or if it must be purchased separately to achieve the desired flush look.

## Is it A Wise Decision to Buy built in microwave oven?

Investing in a built-in microwave oven is a strategic decision for any homeowner looking to modernize their kitchen. Unlike countertop models that clutter your workspaces, built-ins are integrated directly into your cabinetry or islands. This not only creates a professional, high-end aesthetic but also frees up valuable counter space for food preparation.

From a value perspective, built-in units often feature higher-quality construction and more advanced technology like convection heating and air frying. While the initial cost and installation are higher than a standalone unit, the long-term benefits include improved kitchen workflow and a potential increase in home resale value. If you use your microwave daily for more than just reheating coffee, the added convenience of sensor cooking and specialized modes makes it a very wise investment for a functional home.

## What Is the Best Way to Choose built in microwave oven?

The selection process should always start with your kitchen layout. Measure your existing cutout three times to ensure accuracy, as even a quarter-inch difference can prevent a proper fit. Once you know the size, decide on the door style. Side-swing doors are traditional, but drop-down or drawer models are often better for specific heights.

Next, evaluate your cooking habits. If you frequently cook from scratch, prioritize a model with Converter or Inverter technology for better texture. If you have children, a mandatory two-step child lock is a non-negotiable safety feature. Finally, look at the finish; fingerprint-resistant stainless steel is highly recommended for maintaining a clean look with minimal effort. Avoid the common mistake of buying a unit without checking the electrical requirements, as some high-power convection models require a dedicated 20-amp circuit.

## FAQ

### What is the best built in microwave oven for beginners?
Beginners should look for a model with intuitive one-touch controls and clear labeling. The GE 2.0 Cubic Ft model is excellent because it features simple express cooking buttons and highly accurate sensors that handle the timing for you. This removes the guesswork from meal prep and ensures consistent results every time you use the machine.

### How much should I spend on built in microwave oven?
A budget of $400 to $700 is the sweet spot for most homeowners. In this range, you get high-quality stainless steel, reliable sensor technology, and enough power for family use. If you want specialized features like a microwave drawer or full convection baking, you should expect to spend between $800 and $1,200 for a premium unit.

### What features are most important in built in microwave oven?
The most important features are Inverter Technology for even heating and Smart Sensors to prevent overcooking. Additionally, check for a Mute Function if you have an open floor plan and a Child Lock if you have little ones. High wattage, typically 1000W to 1200W, is also essential for fast and efficient cooking performance in a busy kitchen.

### How do I maintain my built in microwave oven?
Regular cleaning is key to longevity. Wipe down the interior with a damp cloth and mild soap daily to prevent grease buildup. For stubborn odors, use the odor removal mode if available, or steam a bowl of water with lemon slices for five minutes. Avoid using abrasive pads on the stainless steel exterior to prevent permanent scratching and dulling of the finish.

### Are expensive built in microwave oven worth it?
Yes, expensive models usually offer convection heating and air fry modes which allow the microwave to act as a second oven. They also tend to have superior build quality, such as soft-close doors and motorized drawers. These features provide a much better user experience and can replace multiple small appliances, saving you space and money in the long run.

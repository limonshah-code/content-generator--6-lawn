## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Emma Jones Air Fryer

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before making a purchase, consider how many people you typically cook for and what types of food you enjoy. A single person or a couple might find a 4-quart unit perfect, while a large family will definitely require a 6-quart or even an 8-quart dual-basket model. If you plan to replace your toaster or rotisserie, a multi-functional oven-style air fryer is your best bet.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($X-$Y ranges)
Air fryers generally fall into three price tiers. Budget-friendly models typically range from $50 to $80 and offer basic functionality. Mid-range models between $90 and $150 often include better digital controls, higher wattages, and more presets. Premium models above $150 usually offer massive capacities, specialized technology like TurboBlaze, or elegant designs like French doors.

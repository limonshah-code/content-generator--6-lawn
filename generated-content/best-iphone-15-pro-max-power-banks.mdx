## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Iphone 15 Pro Max Power Banks

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you click buy, think about your typical day. Are you a minimalist who just needs an emergency 20% to get home, or a traveler who won't see a wall outlet for 12 hours? If you use a MagSafe case, a magnetic power bank is incredibly convenient. However, if you have a non-magnetic rugged case, you should look for a wired option like the iWALK or a standard high-capacity brick.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($20-$60 ranges)
You can find decent power banks starting around $20 for basic 5000mAh models. If you want advanced features like Qi2 certification (15W wireless), premium metal materials for cooling, or high-capacity 10000mAh batteries with digital displays, expect to spend between $40 and $60. Investing a little more often gets you better safety certifications and a longer-lasting battery cell.

### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for "Power Delivery" (PD) for the fastest wired charging. For wireless, 7.5W is standard for iPhones, but Qi2 certification is the new gold standard, offering 15W. Another huge feature is pass-through charging, which allows you to charge the power bank and your phone at the same time at night. Finally, a digital percentage display is much more helpful than four blinking dots.

### 4. Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Metal casings (like aluminum alloy) are superior for heat dissipation, which protects your phone's battery health. If choosing plastic, look for fire-retardant materials. Check the "cycle life" of the battery; a good power bank should handle 500+ full charges before its capacity starts to drop noticeably.

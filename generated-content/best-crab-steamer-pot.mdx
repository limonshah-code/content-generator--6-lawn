## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Crab Steamer Pot

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you purchase, consider where you will be cooking. If you have an induction cooktop, you must choose a pot with a magnetic base, such as those from GasOne or SUNHOUSE. Outdoor enthusiasts should look for high-capacity aluminum or steel sets that include a burner. For small indoor meals, a 15-20 quart pot is usually sufficient, while large gatherings might require 40 quarts or more.

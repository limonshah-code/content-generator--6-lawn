## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Greatneck Files Planes

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
The first step in choosing the right tool is identifying what material you will be working with most often. If you are a generalist, a mixed set like the REXBETI 25-piece kit is ideal. However, if you are a woodworker, you may prioritize a smoothing plane or larger wood rasps. For those working with hardened steel or glass, diamond-coated files are a necessity rather than a luxury.

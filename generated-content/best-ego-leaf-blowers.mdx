## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Ego Leaf Blowers

### Find Your Need
Before making a purchase, assess the size and condition of your yard. If you have a small suburban lot with mostly dry leaves, a 530 CFM model is likely sufficient. However, if you deal with large properties, heavy wet leaves, or debris like pine needles, you will benefit significantly from the higher air volume of the 765 or 880 CFM models. Consider whether you need a full kit with a battery or just the bare tool if you are already in the EGO ecosystem.

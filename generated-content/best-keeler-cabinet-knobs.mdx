## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Keeler Cabinet Knobs

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you start shopping, identify where these knobs will be placed. Are you updating a high-moisture bathroom, a high-traffic kitchen, or a bedroom dresser? In kitchens, you need hardware that is easy to clean and ergonomic for frequent use. For furniture, you might prioritize unique designs like the Pebble or Ostia collections. Also, consider if you are replacing existing hardware; if so, make sure the base diameter is large enough to cover any old marks or screw holes.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($2-$15 ranges)
Cabinet knobs vary significantly in price. Basics like the Hickory Hardware 10-packs typically range from $2 to $4 per knob when bought in bulk. Premium designer pieces like those from the Ostia or Pebble collections can cost $10 to $15 per unit. It is important to calculate the total number of knobs needed before choosing a style, as costs can add up quickly in a large kitchen. Bulk packs are almost always more cost-effective for full room renovations.

### Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Pay attention to the projection and diameter. The projection is how far the knob sticks out from the cabinet; a higher projection is easier to grab but can be a snag hazard in tight spaces. The diameter affects the visual scale of the knob relative to the door. Also, look for the mounting hardware type; most knobs use a standard 8-32 screw, which is usually included, but thicker cabinet doors might require longer screws.

### Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction

## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Lawn Mower For St Augustine Grass

Choosing the right equipment for a St. Augustine lawn requires understanding the unique biology of this grass type. It is thick, holds a lot of moisture, and spreads via surface stolons that can easily be damaged by the wrong tools.

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you purchase a mower or maintenance product, evaluate the current state of your lawn. If you have a healthy lawn and want a precision cut, a high-blade count reel mower is best. If you have bare spots, you will need plugs or patch kits. St. Augustine grass is a warm-season grass, so all products must be compatible with its specific growth cycle and heat requirements.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($X-$Y ranges)
Maintenance for a premium lawn can range significantly. A quality manual reel mower typically costs between $100 and $200. Repair pods and fertilizers for a standard yard usually fall in the $30 to $100 range. While the upfront cost of premium grass plugs like ProVista is higher, they can save you money in the long run by reducing the need for weed killers and excessive water.

### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications
For mowers, the number of blades is the most critical spec. A 7-blade reel is far superior to a 4-blade reel for dense St. Augustine. For fertilizers and repair kits, look for high nitrogen for growth and iron for color. For herbicides, ensure they are specifically labeled for St. Augustine to avoid accidentally killing your lawn.

### 4. Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Look for mowers made with alloy steel blades that retain their edge, as St. Augustine is a tough grass to cut. For grass plugs, ensure they are farm-grown and shipped in a way that protects the root system. High-quality products should feel substantial and have clear instructions for use in southern climates.

### 5. Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Stick to brands that have a history of southern lawn care. Brands like Scotts, Earthwise, and SodPods have spent years researching how to maintain warm-season turf. Buying from established manufacturers ensures you are getting a product that has been tested in real-world conditions and is backed by customer support.

### 6. Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage
Reel mowers are excellent for those with limited storage space as they are compact and often have foldable handles. When buying large bags of fertilizer, consider whether you have a dry place to store them, as moisture can ruin the product. For grass plugs, remember they must be planted immediately upon arrival, so plan your schedule accordingly.

## Is it A Wise Decision to Buy lawn mower for st augustine grass?

Investing in a specialized lawn mower for st augustine grass is one of the smartest decisions a homeowner can make for their property value. St. Augustine grass is notoriously thick and coarse; using an underpowered or dull mower can lead to ragged edges on the grass blades. These tears are not just unsightly—they act as open wounds that invite fungal diseases and pests. A dedicated tool like a 7-blade reel mower provides a clean cut that helps the grass retain moisture and stay healthy.

Compared to standard rotary mowers, a high-quality reel mower provides a more surgical cut. While the initial investment might seem higher than a budget rotary option, the long-term benefits include a denser lawn that naturally chokes out weeds. This reduces your reliance on expensive chemical herbicides and fertilizers. Furthermore, manual options save you money every year on gas, oil, and engine maintenance. Over five years, a specialized manual mower can pay for itself twice over in saved maintenance costs alone.

## What Is the Best Way to Choose lawn mower for st augustine grass?

To choose the best lawn mower for st augustine grass, you should first measure your total lawn area. For small to medium yards, a manual reel mower is often the most efficient and healthy choice. If your yard is larger than half an acre, you might need to look into high-end battery or gas-powered rotary mowers with sharp, high-lift blades.

1. **Check Blade Count:** Always aim for at least 7 blades if going with a reel mower.
2. **Verify Height Settings:** St. Augustine prefers to be kept at 2.5 to 4 inches. Ensure your mower can handle these higher settings.
3. **Analyze Your Terrain:** If your yard has many hills, look for a lightweight mower.
4. **Consider Ease of Maintenance:** Manual mowers are easiest, requiring only seasonal sharpening.
5. **Read Regional Reviews:** Look for feedback from other users in southern states to see how the product handles high humidity and heat.

Avoid the common mistake of buying a mower designed for thin, northern grasses like Fescue. These mowers will often clog or bog down in the thick stolons of a St. Augustine lawn. If you are on a budget, it is better to buy a high-quality manual mower than a cheap, low-power gas mower that will struggle to provide a clean cut.

## FAQ

**What is the best lawn mower for st augustine grass for beginners?**
For beginners, a high-quality manual reel mower like the Earthwise 7-blade model is excellent. It is simple to operate, requires no complex engine maintenance, and provides a very clean cut that is forgiving if you aren't an expert at lawn care yet. It allows you to feel the grass and adjust your pace accordingly.

**How much should I spend on lawn mower for st augustine grass?**
You should expect to spend between $130 and $200 for a quality manual mower. If you are looking at motorized options, the price can jump to $400 or more. For maintenance products like fertilizer and repair pods, a budget of $50 to $100 per season is typical for a standard-sized yard.

## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Lawn Mower Under 700

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you swipe your card, take a good look at your yard. Do you have a small, flat urban plot or a sprawling half-acre with hills? If you have steep terrain, a self-propelled model is non-negotiable to save your back. If you have many obstacles like trees or flower beds, a smaller 13-inch or 17-inch deck will provide better maneuverability than a wide 21-inch model.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($300-$700 ranges)
In the sub-700 category, you have three tiers. The $300-$400 range usually gets you a solid push electric mower or a basic gas model. The $400-$600 range is the sweet spot where you find self-propelled electric models with better battery life. At the $600-$700 mark, you are looking at premium brands like EGO or entry-level robotic mowers that offer high-end tech features.

### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Focus on the motor type and power source. Brushless motors are far superior to brushed ones because they are more efficient, last longer, and provide more torque. If going electric, look at the Amp-hour (Ah) rating of the batteries; higher Ah means longer runtime. For gas mowers, check the engine displacement (cc); 140cc is standard, while 170cc or higher is great for thick grass.

### 4. Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Deck material is a major factor. Steel decks are incredibly durable and can withstand rock strikes, but they can rust if not maintained. Plastic or composite decks will never rust and are much lighter, making them easier to push, though they can be more prone to cracking over many years. Check the warranty—reputable brands offer at least 3 to 5 years of protection.

### 5. Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
In the world of lawn care, parts availability matters. Established brands like EGO, Greenworks, and PowerSmart have robust customer support and easily found replacement blades or batteries. Buying from a recognized name ensures that if a charger fails in three years, you can actually find a replacement rather than having to buy a whole new mower.

### 6. Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage
If you live in a home with a small shed or a crowded garage, look for mowers with vertical storage capabilities. Many modern electric mowers have handles that fold completely flat, allowing the mower to stand on its rear end. This can save up to 70 percent of floor space compared to a traditional gas mower that must stay flat to avoid oil leaks.

## Is it A Wise Decision to Buy lawn mower under 700?

Investing in a lawn mower under 700 is one of the most practical decisions a homeowner can make. This price range represents the perfect intersection of professional-grade performance and residential affordability. While commercial mowers can easily cost thousands, the technology in the sub-700 bracket has advanced so rapidly that most users will not notice a difference in the final look of their lawn.

By choosing a high-quality electric or gas mower in this range, you are securing a tool that will likely last 10 to 15 years with basic maintenance. The long-term savings are significant, especially with electric models where you eliminate the recurring costs of gas, oil, and spark plugs. Furthermore, a well-maintained lawn increases property value and curb appeal, making the mower an investment in your home's equity. Compared to paying a professional landscaping service, most mowers in this price range will pay for themselves in less than a single season of use.

## What Is the Best Way to Choose lawn mower under 700?

Choosing the right mower requires a step-by-step approach to ensure you do not overspend on features you do not need. First, measure your actual lawn area. For anything under a quarter acre, a standard push mower is fine. For larger areas, prioritize battery capacity or gas power. Second, check your grass type. If you have thick, wet, or fast-growing grass, you will need the high torque of an 80V electric system or a 170cc gas engine.

Professional tip: Always look at the battery ecosystem. If you already own power tools from a specific brand, staying within that battery family can save you hundreds of dollars. Avoid the common mistake of buying a mower based solely on the lowest price; a cheap mower that lacks the power to cut your specific grass will end up costing more when you have to replace it a year later. Finally, consider your physical comfort. If you have any mobility issues, always opt for a lightweight model or one with a robust self-propulsion system.

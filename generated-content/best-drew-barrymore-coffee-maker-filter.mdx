## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Drew Barrymore Coffee Maker Filter

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before making a purchase, it is vital to identify exactly which type of filter your Drew Barrymore machine requires. Most "Beautiful" line coffee makers use standard charcoal water filter pods that sit in the reservoir, but they also require a #4 cone filter for the coffee grounds. If your goal is to improve the taste of the water, focus on charcoal pods. If you want to eliminate paper waste and improve the texture of your coffee, look for a reusable mesh cone filter.

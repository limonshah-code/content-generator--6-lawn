## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Kids Horse Riding Helmet

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before purchasing, consider where your child will be riding. If they are just doing pony rides at a local fair, a basic SEI-certified helmet is sufficient. However, if they are enrolled in regular lessons or plan to enter jumping competitions, you need a helmet with ASTM certification and advanced ventilation. Also, check if the helmet is compatible with riding glasses or hair nets if your child uses them.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($30-$80 ranges)
Helmets generally fall into two price brackets. Basic toddler and entry-level helmets typically range from $30 to $50. These are safe and functional but may lack advanced adjustment dials. Professional-grade schooling and competition helmets usually range from $60 to $100. Investing more often gets you better airflow, a more robust ABS shell, and a finer degree of adjustability.

### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Safety certifications (SEI and ASTM) are non-negotiable. Beyond safety, look for a DialFit or YKK adjustment system. This allows the helmet to grow with the child. Ventilation is also critical; look for a minimum of 4-6 vents to prevent heat exhaustion. A removable and washable liner is a massive bonus for hygiene.

### 4. Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
High-grade ABS plastic is more durable and impact-resistant than PVC. Check the straps; they should be securely anchored and made of soft webbing that won't chafe. A matte or Duratec finish is better for longevity as it hides scratches better than a glossy finish which can look worn quickly.

### 5. Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Stick to brands that specialize in equestrian gear, like Ovation or Troxel. These companies understand the specific types of impacts involved in horse riding (which are different from bike riding). Established brands also provide better customer support and more accurate sizing guides.

### 6. Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage
Most helmets come with a storage bag or are small enough to fit in a standard locker. Ensure you measure your child's head accurately (1 inch above the eyebrows) to get the right size. A helmet that is too large is a major safety hazard, while one that is too small will cause headaches and discomfort.

## Is it A Wise Decision to Buy kids horse riding helmet?

Investing in a dedicated kids horse riding helmet is one of the wisest safety decisions you can make. Unlike bike helmets, which are designed for a single forward impact, equestrian helmets are engineered to protect against falls from height and potential impacts from hooves. The specialized design covers more of the back of the head, providing critical protection for the cerebellum.

While some might consider using a multi-sport helmet, the unique physics of a horse-related fall require the specific absorption qualities found in SEI and ASTM certified riding gear. Beyond safety, a high-quality helmet improves the riding experience by keeping the child cool and comfortable. A comfortable child is a more focused student, which leads to better progress in their riding skills. Over the long term, a durable helmet saves money by lasting through several years of growth and use, making the initial investment highly cost-effective compared to replacing cheaper, less reliable gear.

## What Is the Best Way to Choose kids horse riding helmet?

The selection process should always start with an accurate measurement. Use a soft tape measure to find the circumference of the head about an inch above the eyebrows. This measurement will dictate which size category (XS, S, M, L) you need to browse. Once you have the size, filter your options based on safety certifications.

Step-by-step selection guide:
1. Measure the head circumference in centimeters and inches.
2. Filter for SEI/ASTM certified helmets only.
3. Determine the use case (casual pony rides vs. formal lessons).
4. Look for adjustment features like a rear dial or adjustable chin straps.
5. Check for adequate ventilation based on your local climate.
6. Choose a style or color that the child feels confident wearing.

Avoid the common mistake of buying a helmet for a child to 'grow into.' A helmet that is too big can slide over the eyes or fall off during an impact. If you are between sizes, choose a model with a dial-fit system that can be tightened for a secure fit now and loosened as they grow.

## FAQ

**What is the best kids horse riding helmet for beginners?**
For beginners, the Ovation Deluxe Schooler is an excellent choice. It offers professional safety standards and a highly adjustable dial-fit system. This ensures the helmet stays secure even if the child is still learning how to balance. It is lightweight, making it less intimidating for a first-time rider.

**How much should I spend on kids horse riding helmet?**
You should expect to spend between $35 and $75. Entry-level helmets that meet all safety standards are available around the $40 mark. If you want better ventilation and a more durable ABS shell, spending closer to $70 is recommended for long-term value and comfort.

**What features are most important in kids horse riding helmet?**
The most important features are SEI/ASTM safety certifications, an adjustable fit system (like a dial or YKK buckle), and effective ventilation. A removable, washable liner is also highly important for maintaining hygiene over years of use in a dusty barn environment.

**How do I maintain my kids horse riding helmet?**
Keep the helmet clean by wiping the outer shell with a damp cloth. If it has a removable liner, wash it by hand with mild soap and air dry it. Avoid leaving the helmet in a hot car, as extreme heat can degrade the protective foam inside the shell.

**Are expensive kids horse riding helmet worth it?**
Yes, expensive helmets often provide superior materials like ABS plastic, better airflow, and more precise adjustment systems. While all certified helmets are safe, the more expensive ones are usually more comfortable for the rider, which can significantly improve their performance and enjoyment.

**What brands make the best kids horse riding helmet?**
Ovation and Troxel are widely considered the top brands for kids' equestrian gear. They have decades of experience in the industry and consistently meet the highest safety standards while offering styles that appeal to young riders and professional trainers alike.

**How long do kids horse riding helmet typically last?**
A helmet should be replaced every 5 years or immediately after any significant impact. Even if there is no visible damage, the internal foam can be compressed or degraded, losing its ability to absorb shock during a future fall.

**Can I find good kids horse riding helmet on a budget?**
Absolutely. Brands like Xiaozxwlhq offer SEI-certified helmets at a lower price point. While they might use PVC instead of ABS shells, they still provide the legal safety requirements necessary to protect your child during their initial riding experiences.

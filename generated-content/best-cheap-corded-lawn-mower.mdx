## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Cheap Corded Lawn Mower

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you purchase, assess the size and layout of your yard. Corded mowers are best for lawns under 1/4 acre because you are limited by the length of your extension cord. If you have many trees or obstacles, managing the cord can become a chore. However, for a standard rectangular or square lawn, a corded model is virtually unbeatable in terms of value and runtime.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($80-$200 ranges)
Budget mowers typically fall into the $80 to $200 range. At the lower end ($80-$120), you can expect smaller 12 to 14-inch decks and 10-amp motors. As you move toward $180-$200, you gain wider decks (19-20 inches), more powerful 13-amp motors, and additional features like single-lever height adjustment or better collection bags.

### Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for the amperage of the motor; an 11-amp or 13-amp motor will handle thicker grass much better than an 8-amp motor. Deck width is also crucial, as a wider deck means fewer passes and less time spent mowing. Also, check the height adjustment range to ensure it can cut your specific type of grass at the recommended health height.

### Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
While many cheap mowers use plastic decks to save weight and cost, ensure the plastic is high-impact and UV-resistant. Steel decks are more durable but can rust if not maintained. Check the wheel attachments and the handle folding mechanism, as these are common points of failure on budget models.

### Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Buying from established brands like BLACK+DECKER, CRAFTSMAN, or American Lawn Mower Company ensures that you can find replacement parts like blades or bags in the future. These companies also tend to offer better warranties and customer support than generic off-brand imports.

### Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage

Investing in a corded lawn mower is one of the smartest financial moves a homeowner with a small yard can make. Furthermore, corded mowers are much more reliable than cheap battery-powered ones, as batteries degrade over time and are expensive to replace.

## What Is the Best Way to Choose cheap corded lawn mower?

1. **Measure Your Lawn:** Ensure your lawn is within 100 feet of an outdoor power outlet, as this is the maximum safe length for a high-quality extension cord.
2. **Determine Grass Type:** If you have thick, tough grass like St. Augustine, look specifically for a 13-amp motor. For thinner grasses, an 11-amp motor is sufficient.
3. **Check Storage Space:** Measure your shed or garage corner. If space is tight, look for a model with vertical storage or fully folding handles.
4. **Read Real Reviews:** Look for feedback regarding how the mower handles damp grass and how easy it is to empty the collection bag.
5. **Buy the Right Cord:** Never use a standard indoor extension cord. You must buy a 12-gauge or 14-gauge outdoor-rated cord to prevent motor burnout.

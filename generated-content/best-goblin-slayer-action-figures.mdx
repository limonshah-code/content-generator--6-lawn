## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Goblin Slayer Action Figures

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you make a purchase, decide how you plan to use your figure. Are you a toy photographer who needs high articulation like the Figma or Doom Slayer models? Or are you a display collector who prefers the elegant, static look of a 1:7 scale PVC statue? If you want something for your desk that won't break if it falls, a plush or an entry-level articulated figure might be the best choice. Consider your shelf space as well, as some bases are much wider than others.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($20-$200 ranges)
Action figures vary widely in price. Entry-level 5-inch figures typically range from $20 to $40. Moving up to Figma or standard PVC statues, you can expect to pay between $60 and $120. Premium, large-scale 1:6 or 1:7 figures often fall in the $150 to $250 range. Setting a budget helps narrow down your choices between high-end collectibles and more affordable, playable options.

### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for the number of articulation points if you want posing versatility; 22 to 27 points is excellent for a 5-to-7-inch figure. For statues, check the scale (1:7 is standard, 1:6 is larger) and the material (PVC is the industry standard for durability and detail). Accessories are also key—does it come with extra hands, weapons, or a removable hat? These features add significant value to your purchase.

### 4. Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Check for solid joints that aren't loose, and look for paint work that is clean with no overspill. Higher-quality figures use premium PVC that doesn't smell or warp over time. For articulated figures, double-jointed shoulders and knees are signs of superior engineering. Weight can also be an indicator of quality; heavier statues are often more stable and better constructed.

### 5. Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Stick to well-known brands like Figma (Max Factory), Kadokawa, Phat!, and Bandai. These companies are officially licensed, meaning their designs are approved by the original creators and they adhere to safety and quality standards. Buying from reputable brands also ensures better resale value and more accurate character likenesses compared to knock-off versions.

### 6. Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage
The size of the figure affects where it can live in your home. A 5-inch figure is great for a computer desk, while a 1:6 scale Priestess with a 10-inch base will require a dedicated shelf or display case. Also, consider the packaging—if you move often, keeping the original boxes is important for safe transport, so ensure you have space to store those as well.

## What to Look for Before Buying the Best 21 Self Propelled Lawn Mower

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
The first thing you must evaluate is the size and terrain of your property. If you have a small, flat lawn, a lower voltage 40V model will be more than sufficient and lighter to maneuver. However, if your yard is over a half-acre or contains steep inclines, you should prioritize a 60V or 80V self-propelled model. Gas mowers are best for those with very large properties where stopping to charge a battery is not practical. Also, consider the storage situation; many modern electric mowers can be stored vertically, which is a huge benefit for those with limited garage space.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($300-$800 ranges)
Self-propelled mowers typically fall into the $300 to $800 range. At the lower end ($300-$450), you will find reliable 40V models or basic gas-powered units. The mid-range ($450-$600) often includes high-capacity 60V models with dual batteries or powerful 201cc gas engines. Premium models ($600-$800) feature 80V systems, smart pace technology, and brushless motors with long warranties.

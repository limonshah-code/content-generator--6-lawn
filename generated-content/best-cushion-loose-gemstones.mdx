### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Pay close attention to the 4Cs: Color, Cut, Clarity, and Carat weight. In a cushion cut, the quality of the faceting is crucial because it determines how well the stone hides inclusions and reflects light. Look for "eye-clean" stones where inclusions aren't visible to the naked eye.

### 4. Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Always check the Mohs hardness of the mineral. Diamonds are a 10, sapphires and rubies are a 9, and zircons or garnets are typically lower. The cushion cut should have even corners and a depth that isn't too shallow, or the stone will look dull.

### 5. Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
When buying high-value stones, purchase from reputable sellers like GM GemMartUSA or specialized gemstone marts that provide certification. A certificate from a recognized laboratory ensures the stone's origin and treatment history are accurately disclosed.

### 6. Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage
Consider the physical dimensions in millimeters, not just the carat weight. A 10-carat cushion stone can be quite large (over 12mm) and may require a custom-made setting rather than a standard "off-the-shelf" mount. Ensure your jeweler can accommodate the stone's specific depth.

## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Ego Lawn Mower

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before making a purchase, assess your yard's terrain and size. If you have steep hills, a self-propelled model with Touch Drive is essential to save your back. For smaller, flat yards, a push model might be more efficient and provide a better workout. Also, consider if you already own EGO tools, as buying a tool-only version can save you hundreds of dollars.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($400-$6,000)
EGO mowers sit in the premium segment of the market. A basic push mower (tool only) might start around $400, while high-end self-propelled kits with large batteries range from $700 to $1,000. If you are looking at riding mowers, expect to invest between $4,500 and $6,000. Remember to factor in the long-term savings from not buying gas or oil.

### Key Features - Most important technical specifications

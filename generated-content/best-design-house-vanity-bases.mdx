## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Design House Vanity Bases

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before making a purchase, measure your bathroom space multiple times. Consider who will be using the vanity most. A master bathroom might require a 48-inch or 72-inch double bowl setup, while a guest powder room only needs a compact 24-inch unit. Ensure the plumbing placement in your wall aligns with the cabinet's internal layout to avoid difficult modifications.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($150-$800 ranges)
Vanity bases can range from $150 for basic RTA (Ready to Assemble) models to over $800 for solid wood, fully assembled units. Generally, lighting fixtures will fall between $50 and $150. Remember to factor in the cost of the vanity top, faucet, and installation materials, as these are rarely included with the base itself.

### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for soft-close hinges and drawer glides to enhance the user experience and protect the cabinet. Dovetail drawer construction is a sign of high-quality woodworking that will last longer than stapled alternatives. If you are choosing lighting, ensure it is ETL-rated for damp locations to guarantee safety in a humid bathroom environment.

### 4. Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Solid wood is the gold standard for durability, especially in high-moisture areas. If you choose an MDF or particle board option, ensure it has a high-quality finish like Sherwin Williams paint to prevent water from seeping into the core. Check for 6-way adjustable hinges which allow you to keep the doors perfectly level over time.

### 5. Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Design House has over 150 years of experience, which is a major advantage. Established brands offer better customer support, more reliable warranties, and consistent part availability. Choosing a reputable brand ensures that if a knob breaks or a hinge fails, you can easily find a replacement that fits perfectly.

### 6. Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage

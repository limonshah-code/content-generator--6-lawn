## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Hand Grinder For Espresso 2

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before purchasing, consider where you will be using your grinder most. If you are a traveler or hiker, weight and size are paramount, making the 30g mini models ideal. However, if this is for your home kitchen and you often brew for more than one person, a 60g or 80g model will save you significant time and frustration. Also, check if the grinder fits your specific espresso machine filter size if you plan to grind directly into it.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($X-$Y ranges)
Manual grinders generally fall into three price tiers. The entry-level tier ($20-$40) usually features ceramic burrs and plastic components. The mid-range tier ($50-$90) introduces stainless steel burrs, metal bodies, and bearing systems for smoother rotation. The high-end tier ($100+) offers specialized burr geometries like Spike-to-Cut and extreme precision. For espresso, spending more on a mid-range or high-end unit usually pays off in better shot quality.

### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications

## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Cordless Vacuums For 200

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you choose, look at your home layout. Do you have mostly hardwood floors or high-pile carpets? If you have thick carpets, you need a model with higher suction power or a specialized brush roll. If you have pets, anti-tangle technology is almost a requirement to avoid frustration.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($100-$200 ranges)
In the sub-200 dollar range, you are looking for a balance of power and battery life. While you might not get the smart sensors found in 600 dollar models, you can find excellent suction and 40 to 60 minutes of battery life. Avoid anything under 100 dollars if you have pets, as they often lack the motor strength needed for hair.

### Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for runtime and battery type. Lithium-ion is standard and provides fade-free power. Also, check for LED lights on the floor head; they might seem like a gimmick, but they are essential for seeing dust under furniture. Finally, consider if you want a self-standing model or if you are okay with a wall mount.

### Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction

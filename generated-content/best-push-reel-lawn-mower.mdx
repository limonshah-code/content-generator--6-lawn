## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Push Reel Lawn Mower

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you purchase, assess the size and type of your lawn. If you have a small, flat yard, a 14-inch mower is easy to maneuver. Larger yards benefit from 18-inch or 20-inch models to reduce the number of passes. Also, consider your grass type; dense grasses like Bermuda may require a 7-blade reel for the best results.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($80-$160 ranges)
Push reel mowers are generally more affordable than gas or electric versions. Entry-level models typically range from $80 to $100. If you want features like wider decks, more height adjustments, or included grass catchers, expect to spend between $120 and $160. This investment pays for itself quickly since there are no ongoing fuel or battery costs.

### Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for the number of blades and the wheel size. A 5-blade reel is standard for most lawns, while 4-blade models are lighter and better for thin grass. Large 10-inch wheels provide better stability and are easier to push over uneven terrain. Ball-bearing reels are also a must for a smooth spinning action that reduces physical effort.

### Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Check for heat-treated or tempered alloy steel blades, as these will stay sharp for several seasons. Avoid models with too much plastic in the cutting assembly. Steel side plates and a solid handle construction are indicators that the mower will last through years of weekly use without falling apart.

### Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Stick with brands that have a history in manual lawn care, such as Scotts or American Lawn Mower Company. These brands offer better access to replacement parts and sharpening instructions. Choosing a reputable brand ensures that the reel and bed knife are properly aligned right out of the box.

### Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage
One of the main benefits of a reel mower is its compact size. Many models allow you to hang them on a wall or fold the handles. If you have limited garage space, check the weight and handle design to ensure you can store it conveniently when not in use.

## Is it A Wise Decision to Buy push reel lawn mower?

Investing in a push reel lawn mower is an excellent decision for those who value both their lawn health and the environment. Unlike traditional rotary mowers that chop and tear at the grass, a reel mower uses a scissor-like action. This precise cut allows the grass to heal faster and prevents disease, resulting in a greener and more resilient lawn.

Beyond the horticultural benefits, the long-term cost savings are significant. You will never have to buy gasoline, change oil, replace spark plugs, or deal with battery degradation. The only maintenance required is an occasional spray of lubricant and a blade sharpening every few years. It is also a silent alternative, allowing you to mow at any time of day without bothering your family or neighbors. While it does require more physical effort than a self-propelled mower, many homeowners find the exercise to be a positive benefit of their weekly yard work.

## What Is the Best Way to Choose push reel lawn mower?

Choosing the right mower starts with measuring your typical grass height. If you often miss a week of mowing, look for a model like the Scotts 2000-20S that can handle grass up to 3 inches tall. Most reel mowers struggle with very tall grass, so choosing one with a high max adjustment is vital for busy schedules.

Next, consider the physical weight of the machine. If you need to carry the mower up stairs or hang it high, a lightweight 14-inch model like the American Lawn Mower 1204-14 is best. However, if your yard is open and flat, the momentum of a heavier 20-inch mower actually makes it easier to keep moving once you start. Avoid the mistake of buying a 7-blade mower for tall, thin grass; these are specifically meant for short, dense turf. Always check that the handle is adjustable or cushioned if you plan on spending more than 30 minutes mowing at a time.

## FAQ

**What is the best push reel lawn mower for beginners?**
For beginners, we recommend a 14-inch or 16-inch model with 5 blades. These are lightweight enough to be easily managed while providing a clean cut on standard grass types. The American Lawn Mower 1415-16 is a perfect starting point because it is easy to assemble and requires very little maintenance.

**How much should I spend on push reel lawn mower?**
Most homeowners should expect to spend between $90 and $140. This price range gets you a durable model with alloy steel blades and adjustable height settings. While there are cheaper options, spending a bit more ensures the blades stay sharp longer and the wheels glide more smoothly.

**What features are most important in push reel lawn mower?**
The most important features are the cutting width, blade material, and wheel size. A wider deck saves time, while alloy steel blades ensure longevity. Large 10-inch wheels make the mower much easier to push over small bumps or through thicker patches of grass.

**How do I maintain my push reel lawn mower?**
Maintenance is simple. After each use, wipe down the blades to remove moisture and grass clippings. Apply a light coating of WD-40 or similar lubricant to the reel to prevent rust. You should also check the blade alignment once a year and sharpen them if they start tearing rather than cutting.

**Are expensive push reel lawn mower worth it?**
Yes, if you have a larger yard or specialty grass. More expensive models usually offer wider cutting paths, better ergonomics, and more precise height adjustments. They often use higher-quality bearings that make the reel spin with significantly less effort from the user.

**What brands make the best push reel lawn mower?**
American Lawn Mower Company, Scotts, and Great States are the top brands in the industry. They have been producing manual mowers for decades and are known for their durable designs and excellent customer support. LawnMaster is also a strong newer contender with great feature sets.

**How long do push reel lawn mower typically last?**
A well-maintained reel mower can easily last 10 to 15 years. Since they have very few moving parts and no engine, there is very little that can break. As long as you keep the blades sharp and the reel lubricated, it will serve you for a very long time.

**Can I find good push reel lawn mower on a budget?**
Absolutely. The 14-inch models from American Lawn Mower Company are very affordable and offer excellent performance for small yards. Even budget-friendly models use the same basic scissor-cut technology as premium ones, so you still get the benefits of a healthy lawn.

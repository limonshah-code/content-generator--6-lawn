## What Is the Best Way to Choose island for kitchen with sink?

Choosing the best island requires a systematic approach to ensure it fits your lifestyle and space. Start by measuring the exact dimensions where you plan to place the island, leaving at least 36 inches of clearance around all sides for movement. Next, identify your primary water source; most portable islands connect via a garden hose, so proximity to an outdoor faucet is crucial.

Professional tips suggest looking for units with six wheels if you plan to place heavy ceramic grills on them, as four wheels can sometimes buckle under extreme weight over time. Avoid the common mistake of ignoring the sink depth; a shallow sink will cause more splashing and make it difficult to wash larger items. If you are on a budget, prioritize the quality of the sink and countertop over extra storage accessories, as these are the parts that will see the most wear. Lastly, always check if the faucet is included or if you need to purchase a compatible one separately to avoid delays during setup.

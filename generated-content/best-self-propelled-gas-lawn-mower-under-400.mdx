## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Self Propelled Gas Lawn Mower Under 400

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you purchase, assess the size and layout of your yard. If you have a relatively flat yard with many obstacles like trees or flower beds, a front-wheel drive (FWD) model might be best because it allows you to pivot the machine easily. However, if your yard has hills or slopes, a rear-wheel drive (RWD) mower is essential for traction. Additionally, consider how much you value grass management options. If you want a perfectly manicured look without manual cleanup, ensure the mower has a high-quality mulching or bagging system.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($250-$400 ranges)
In the $250 to $400 price bracket, you are looking at mid-range gas mowers that offer self-propulsion. At the lower end ($250-$300), you will find reliable engines from smaller brands and standard wheel sizes. As you approach the $400 mark, you gain access to premium engines like Briggs and Stratton, wider cutting decks (22 inches), and high-wheel designs that handle rough terrain better. Setting a budget within this range allows for a machine that is much more durable than the entry-level push models.

### Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Focus on engine displacement (cc) and deck width. For most residential yards, a 140cc to 170cc engine provides sufficient power. A wider deck (21-22 inches) will save you time by reducing the number of passes required. Look for "auto-choke" or "ReadyStart" features to eliminate the frustration of manual priming. Also, check the height adjustment settings; a single-lever system is much faster to use than adjusting each wheel individually.

### Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
A steel deck is the gold standard for durability in this price range. While some high-end mowers use composite materials to resist rust, steel provides the weight and strength needed to handle debris. Look for reinforced handle attachments and high-quality wheel bearings. Even in the budget category, the thickness of the steel deck matters; a "stamped" steel deck should feel rigid and not flex when you apply pressure.

### Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Brands like CRAFTSMAN and PowerSmart have established histories in the outdoor power equipment space. Buying from a recognized brand often means better access to replacement parts like blades, belts, and air filters. It also ensures that you have a warranty path should something go wrong. While newer brands can offer great value, always check for customer service availability in your region.

### Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage
Self-propelled mowers can be heavy and bulky. If you have limited space in your garage or shed, look for models with foldable handles. Some modern gas mowers are even designed for "vertical storage," allowing them to be tipped up without oil or fuel leaking into the air filter. Check the dimensions when the handle is folded to ensure it will fit in your designated storage area.

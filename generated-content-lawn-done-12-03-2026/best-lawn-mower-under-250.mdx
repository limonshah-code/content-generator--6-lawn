## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Lawn Mower Under 250

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you purchase, assess the size and terrain of your property. If you have a small, flat city lot, a lightweight electric or corded mower with a 13 to 15-inch deck is usually sufficient. However, if your yard exceeds half an acre or has significant hills, you should prioritize a self-propelled gas mower or a high-voltage cordless model with at least a 20-inch deck. Consider also if you need to trim edges, in which case a combo kit might be the most logical choice.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($100-$250 ranges)
In the sub-250 price range, you are typically choosing between high-end corded models, mid-range 40V cordless mowers, or entry-level gas mowers. Models closer to 100 are often corded or have smaller decks, while those approaching the 250 limit offer features like brushless motors, self-propelled drives, or dual-battery systems. Always account for the cost of a high-quality outdoor extension cord if choosing a corded model.

### Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for brushless motors in electric models, as they provide better torque and longer life. Height adjustment is also crucial; look for at least 5 positions to accommodate different grass types and seasons. Cutting width is another factor—larger decks (19-21 inches) save time but are harder to store, while smaller decks (13-16 inches) are easier to maneuver around flower beds.

### Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Check the material of the deck. Steel decks are more durable and provide better stability but are heavier and can rust if not maintained. Plastic or composite decks are lighter and rust-proof but can crack if you hit large rocks. Also, inspect the wheel quality; larger rear wheels (8 inches or more) make the mower significantly easier to push over bumpy terrain.

### Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Buying from a reputable brand like Greenworks, LawnMaster, or PowerSmart ensures you can find replacement parts like blades and batteries in the future. Established brands also tend to offer better customer support and longer warranties. A 2 or 3-year warranty is standard for quality mowers in this price range and provides peace of mind regarding your investment.

### Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage
If you have a small garage or shed, prioritize mowers with foldable or vertical storage capabilities. Lightweight mowers (under 40 lbs) are also much easier to lift into a car or carry up basement stairs. Check if the grass bag is collapsible as well, as this further reduces the storage footprint during the winter months.

## What to Look for Before Buying the Best 21 Electric Lawn Mower

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before making a purchase, assess the size and terrain of your lawn. If you have a small, flat yard, a push model like the PowerSmart 80V might save you money and complexity. However, if your property has hills or is larger than a quarter-acre, a self-propelled model like the EGO Power+ or Greenworks 80V is almost essential to prevent fatigue. Also, consider the grass type; thicker grasses like St. Augustine require higher voltage systems (60V or 80V) to avoid stalling.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($350-$800 ranges)
Electric mowers generally cost more upfront than their gas counterparts, but they save money on fuel and maintenance over time. Budget-friendly models typically fall in the $350-$450 range and often feature 40V systems. Mid-range models ($500-$650) offer higher voltages like 60V and self-propelled features. Premium models ($700+) provide the best battery life, advanced drive systems, and multi-blade technology for a superior finish.

### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for brushless motors, which are more efficient and last longer than brushed versions. Battery capacity, measured in Amp-hours (Ah), determines how long you can mow. For a 21-inch mower, aim for at least a 5.0Ah battery or dual 4.0Ah batteries. Another critical feature is the discharge system; ensure the mower offers 3-in-1 functionality (mulching, bagging, and side discharge) to handle different seasonal needs.

### 4. Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Deck material is a primary factor in durability. Steel decks, found on many Greenworks and PowerSmart models, are heavy-duty and resist impacts but can rust if not cleaned. Plastic (polymer) decks, common on EGO and some WORX models, are lighter and completely rust-proof. Also, check the handle construction; aluminum handles offer a more rigid feel and better corrosion resistance than thin steel tubing.

### 5. Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
In the electric mower world, brands like Greenworks, EGO, and WORX are the leaders. Choosing a reputable brand is crucial because batteries are often proprietary. By sticking to one ecosystem, you can share batteries between your mower, leaf blower, and string trimmer. Established brands also offer better warranty support and easier access to replacement parts like blades and bags.

### 6. Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage
One of the greatest benefits of electric mowers is the ability to store them vertically. Look for models with fully collapsible handles and specialized frames that allow the mower to stand on its rear wheels. This can reduce the storage footprint by up to 70 percent. Weight is also a factor; if you need to lift the mower into a vehicle or up stairs, look for models with integrated lift handles.

## Is it A Wise Decision to Buy 21 electric lawn mower?

Investing in a 21-inch electric lawn mower is an increasingly wise decision for the modern homeowner. Traditional gas mowers are notorious for their loud noise, messy fuel storage, and the constant need for oil changes, spark plug replacements, and air filter cleanings. Electric mowers eliminate all of these headaches. With a push-button start, you no longer have to struggle with a pull-cord, which is a significant benefit for users of all ages and physical abilities.

While the initial purchase price might be higher, the total cost of ownership is significantly lower. You will save hundreds of dollars over the life of the mower by avoiding gasoline and engine maintenance. Furthermore, these mowers are much better for the environment, producing zero emissions at the point of use. If you have a standard residential yard, the current state of battery technology ensures that you can finish your entire lawn on a single charge. The quiet operation also means you can mow at your convenience without disturbing your family or neighbors, making it a socially responsible and personally convenient investment.

## What Is the Best Way to Choose 21 electric lawn mower?

To choose the best model for your specific situation, follow these steps:

1. **Measure Your Lawn:** For lawns under 1/4 acre, a 40V push model is usually sufficient. For 1/2 acre or more, look for 60V or 80V self-propelled models with dual battery ports.
2. **Check Your Current Tool Shed:** If you already own WORX or Greenworks cordless tools, it makes sense to stay within that battery ecosystem to save money on future tool purchases.
3. **Decide on Drive Type:** If you are physically fit and have a flat lawn, save money with a push model. If you have hills or physical limitations, a self-propelled model is worth the extra cost.
4. **Evaluate Storage Space:** If garage space is at a premium, prioritize models with vertical storage capabilities.
5. **Research Cut Quality:** Look for features like EGO's multi-blade system or WORX's Aerodeck if you want a lawn that looks professionally manicured.
6. **Consider the Time of Day:** If you prefer mowing in the evening or early morning, ensure the model you choose has LED headlights.

For a reliable 21-inch model that will last several years, expect to spend between $450 and $600. This range usually includes a high-capacity battery and a charger. While you can find cheaper models, they often have smaller decks or lower voltage systems that may struggle with thick grass.

**What features are most important in 21 electric lawn mower?**
The brushless motor and battery capacity (Ah) are the most important. A brushless motor provides more torque and lasts longer. A higher Ah rating means you can mow for a longer duration. Self-propulsion and vertical storage are also highly recommended for comfort and convenience.

**How do I maintain my 21 electric lawn mower?**
Maintenance is minimal. The most important tasks are keeping the blade sharp and cleaning the underside of the deck after each use to prevent grass buildup. You should also store the batteries in a cool, dry place and avoid leaving them on the charger for weeks at a time during the off-season.

**Are expensive 21 electric lawn mower worth it?**
Yes, expensive models like the EGO Power+ offer significantly better cut quality due to multi-blade systems and faster charging. They also tend to have better warranties and more durable construction. If you have a difficult lawn or want the best possible result, the premium is worth it.

**How long do 21 electric lawn mower typically last?**
A well-maintained electric mower can last 8 to 10 years. The batteries typically last 3 to 5 years (or 500-1000 charge cycles) before they start to lose capacity. Most manufacturers offer 3 to 5-year warranties on the tools and batteries.

Yes, models like the PowerSmart 80V push mower offer high power at a more accessible price point by removing the self-propelled drive system. Look for sales on 40V models during the spring and autumn for the best deals.

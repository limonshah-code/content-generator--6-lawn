## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Greenworks Lawn Mower

### 1. Find Your Need
Before selecting a model, evaluate the size and terrain of your yard. If you have a small townhome lot under 1/4 acre, a 40V 16-inch mower is likely sufficient and easier to store. However, for larger properties or those with significant hills, the 60V or 80V self-propelled models are much better suited to the task. Consider if you want to use the same battery for other tools like trimmers or blowers.

### 2. Budget
Greenworks offers a wide range of price points. Entry-level 40V push mowers typically range from $250 to $400. Mid-range 60V self-propelled models often sit between $450 and $600. High-end 80V commercial-grade or wide-deck models can exceed $700. Factor in the cost of extra batteries if you have a very large property, as batteries are often the most expensive component.

### 3. Key Features
Look for features that make the job easier, such as self-propulsion for hills or SmartCut technology for battery efficiency. LED headlights are great if you mow during twilight hours. Deck material is also important; steel decks are more durable but heavier, while plastic or composite decks are lighter and rust-proof.

### 4. Quality & Durability
Greenworks is known for using high-quality brushless motors in their mid-to-high tier models. These motors last longer and provide more torque than brushed versions. Check the deck construction and handle materials; aluminum handles and steel decks generally indicate a more durable machine that can withstand years of use.

### 5. Brand Reputation
Greenworks is a leader in the cordless outdoor power equipment industry. They offer some of the best warranties in the business, often providing 3 to 4 years of coverage on both the tool and the battery. This level of support is a strong indicator of their commitment to product longevity and customer satisfaction.

### 6. Portability/Size
If you have limited garage space, look for models with vertical storage capabilities. Many Greenworks mowers are designed to fold flat and stand upright, reducing their footprint by up to 70 percent. Weight is also a factor; if you need to lift the mower over curbs or into a vehicle, the 40V models are significantly lighter than the 80V versions.

## Is it A Wise Decision to Buy greenworks lawn mower?

Investing in a Greenworks lawn mower is an excellent decision for the modern homeowner looking to simplify their yard maintenance. Traditional gas mowers require constant upkeep, including oil changes, spark plug replacements, and the frequent purchase of gasoline. In contrast, Greenworks battery-powered mowers are virtually maintenance-free. You simply charge the battery, push a button, and start mowing.

Beyond the convenience, the long-term cost savings are substantial. While the initial purchase price might be higher than some low-end gas models, you will save hundreds of dollars over the years on fuel and engine maintenance. Furthermore, the environmental benefits are undeniable; these machines produce zero emissions at the point of use and operate much more quietly, making them a better choice for your family and your neighbors. With the rapid advancement of battery technology, these mowers now offer performance that rivals or even exceeds traditional gas engines, making the switch a logical and forward-thinking choice.

## What Is the Best Way to Choose greenworks lawn mower?

Choosing the right Greenworks mower involves a step-by-step assessment of your specific situation. Follow this guide to find your match:

1. **Measure Your Lawn:** For yards under 1/4 acre, the 40V line is perfect. For 1/4 to 1/2 acre, look at the 60V or 80V push models. For over 1/2 acre, a 60V or 80V self-propelled model with dual battery ports is recommended.
2. **Check Your Terrain:** If your yard is flat, a push mower is fine and saves money. If you have any significant slopes, the rear-wheel drive self-propelled feature is worth the extra investment to prevent fatigue.
3. **Select Your Platform:** If you already own Greenworks 40V tools, stick with a 40V mower so you can swap batteries. If you are starting fresh, the 60V platform offers the widest variety of high-performance tools for most residential needs.
4. **Consider the Deck Width:** A 21-inch deck is the standard and works for most. If you want to finish even faster, the 25-inch dual-blade model is a fantastic premium option.
5. **Review Storage Options:** If space is tight, ensure the model you choose features the EZ fold handles and vertical storage design.

Common mistakes to avoid include buying a deck that is too small for a large yard or choosing a high-voltage commercial mower for a tiny patch of grass. Always look for brushless motors whenever your budget allows, as they provide a significantly better experience.

## FAQ

**What is the best greenworks lawn mower for beginners?**
The Greenworks 40V 16-inch mower is the best starting point. It is extremely lightweight, easy to assemble, and features a simple push-button start. It removes all the complexity of gas engines while being very affordable. It is perfect for those who have never operated a mower before or are moving from a small apartment to a house with a yard.

**How much should I spend on greenworks lawn mower?**
Most homeowners find their ideal balance of price and performance between $400 and $600. This range typically includes 60V or 80V models with a 21-inch deck and self-propelled capabilities. If you have a very small, flat yard, you can spend as little as $300 and still get a high-quality machine.

**What features are most important in greenworks lawn mower?**
The brushless motor and self-propelled drive are the most impactful features for performance. Brushless motors provide the torque needed for thick grass, while the self-propelled system makes the physical work much easier. Additionally, vertical storage is a highly valued feature for anyone with a crowded garage or shed.

**How do I maintain my greenworks lawn mower?**
Maintenance is incredibly simple. You should keep the underside of the deck clean of grass buildup to maintain airflow. Sharpen the blade once or twice a season to ensure a clean cut. Most importantly, keep your batteries in a cool, dry place and do not store them in extreme heat or freezing temperatures over the winter.

**Are expensive greenworks lawn mower worth it?**
Yes, the higher-end models usually offer 80V power, larger steel decks, and dual-battery systems. These features are essential if you have a large property or very thick, tough grass. The more expensive models also tend to have better build quality, such as aluminum handles and more advanced motor controllers that last longer.

**What brands make the best greenworks lawn mower?**

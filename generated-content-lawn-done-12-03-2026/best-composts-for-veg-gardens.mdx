## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Composts For Veg Gardens

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you purchase any compost, you must determine how you intend to use it. Are you filling a brand new raised bed, or are you just top dressing an existing garden? Inground composters are excellent for direct nutrient delivery, while bagged mixtures are better for large scale soil improvement. Consider your available space and the types of vegetables you plan to grow.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($15-$150 ranges)

## Is it A Wise Decision to Buy composts for veg gardens?

Investing in high quality compost for your vegetable garden is one of the smartest decisions any gardener can make.

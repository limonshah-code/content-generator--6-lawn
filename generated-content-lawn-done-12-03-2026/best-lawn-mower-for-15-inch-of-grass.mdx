## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Lawn Mower For 15 Inch Of Grass

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you purchase, assess your specific lawn type. If you have a flat, standard residential lot, a 15-inch corded or cordless mower is ideal. However, if your yard is uneven or contains thick weeds, you might need the higher RPMs found in the corded models. Consider if you only need to cut the grass or if you also require trimming and edging, in which case a 3-in-1 combo might save you significant time and money.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($X-$Y ranges)
Lawn mowers in this category typically fall between $100 and $400. Manual reel mowers are the most affordable, often under $120. Corded electric models usually range from $130 to $200. The premium cordless models with dual batteries and fast chargers will occupy the $250 to $400 range. Setting a realistic budget helps you decide between the unlimited runtime of a corded unit and the convenience of a battery-powered one.

### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for adjustable height settings; having at least 5 positions allows you to adapt to seasonal grass growth. Cutting width is another factor—while 15 inches is standard for this category, some compact models may offer 12 or 13 inches for better maneuverability. Motor amperage (for corded) and voltage (for cordless) are the primary indicators of cutting power.

### 4. Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Check the deck material. Poly decks are excellent for resisting rust and keeping the machine lightweight, while steel decks offer more ruggedness for rougher terrain. Look at the wheel size as well; larger rear wheels (7 inches or more) typically provide better stability on uneven ground.

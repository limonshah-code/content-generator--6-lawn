## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Inverter Generator

### Find Your Need
Before you make a purchase, determine exactly what you plan to power. If you are a casual camper with a small van, a 2200W unit like the Honda EU2200i or WEN 56360i is likely sufficient. However, if you have a large RV with a 15,000 BTU air conditioner, you will need a unit with at least 3600W to 4000W of surge power. For home backup, look for models with 5000W or higher to ensure you can run critical appliances like your refrigerator and well pump simultaneously.

### Budget
Inverter generators are an investment, typically ranging from $400 for basic models to over $2,500 for high-end home backup systems. 
- **$400 - $600:** Portable gas-only units with manual starts.
- **$700 - $1,200:** Mid-range dual-fuel units with electric starts and wheels.
- **$1,300+:** High-wattage home backup systems or premium brands like Honda.
Setting a realistic budget helps you narrow down features like remote start or tri-fuel capability.

### Key Features
Focus on the technical specifications that will make your life easier. Total Harmonic Distortion (THD) should be below 3% for sensitive electronics. Parallel capability is another great feature, as it allows you to connect two smaller, lighter generators to get the power of one large unit. Look for CO sensors (like WEN Watchdog or Honda CO-Minder) which are becoming standard for safety.

### Quality & Durability
Construction quality is often reflected in the engine displacement (cc) and the materials used for the frame. Cast iron sleeves in the engine help it last longer under heavy use. Look for units that feature weather-resistant outlet covers and sturdy, puncture-proof wheels if the unit is heavy.

### Brand Reputation
Established brands like Honda, Westinghouse, and WEN have extensive service networks. This is crucial because generators require maintenance like oil changes and spark plug replacements. Choosing a reputable brand ensures you can find replacement parts and qualified technicians years down the road.

### Portability/Size
If you plan to move your generator frequently, portability is king. Weight can range from a feather-light 46 pounds to a hefty 200 pounds for large home units. If you choose a heavier model, ensure it has a sturdy telescoping handle and high-quality wheels. Consider the storage space in your garage or RV basement to make sure the unit fits comfortably.

## Is it A Wise Decision to Buy inverter generator?

Investing in an inverter generator is one of the smartest decisions you can make for your home or outdoor lifestyle. Unlike traditional open-frame generators, inverters use advanced circuitry to produce clean, stable power that is as safe as the electricity in your home's wall outlets. This makes them indispensable for anyone who needs to charge laptops, smartphones, or high-end kitchen appliances without risking electrical damage.

While they are generally more expensive than traditional generators, the long-term benefits are substantial. They are significantly more fuel-efficient, as the engine can automatically throttle down when the load is low, saving you money on gas or propane. Additionally, the quiet operation is a massive benefit, allowing you to use them in residential neighborhoods or quiet campgrounds without causing a disturbance. For home backup, the peace of mind knowing that you have a safe, quiet source of power during a blackout is worth every penny of the initial cost.

## What Is the Best Way to Choose inverter generator?

Selecting the right unit requires a systematic approach to ensure you don't overspend or end up with insufficient power. Start by creating a list of every appliance you intend to run. Add up their running watts and identify the highest starting (surge) watts among them to find your minimum required generator size.

Once you have your wattage, decide on your primary fuel source. Dual-fuel models are highly recommended for home backup because propane stores indefinitely, unlike gasoline which can degrade. After narrowing down by fuel and power, look at convenience features. If you have physical limitations, a remote electric start is a must. If you value data, choose a model with an LED screen that shows remaining run time. Finally, read real-world reviews to see how the unit performs in conditions similar to yours, and always check the warranty terms to protect your investment.

## FAQ

**What is the best inverter generator for beginners?**  
The WEN 56360i is an excellent choice for beginners due to its simple operation and lightweight design. It provides enough power for basic needs without the complexity of dual-fuel systems or heavy wheel kits.

**How much should I spend on inverter generator?**  
For a reliable, mid-sized unit (3000W-4000W), expect to spend between $600 and $900. If you need a massive home backup unit, budget around $1,200 to $1,500.

**What features are most important in inverter generator?**  
The most important features are clean power output (low THD), a CO shutdown sensor for safety, and fuel efficiency via an economy mode.

**How do I maintain my inverter generator?**  
Regular maintenance involves changing the oil after the break-in period and then every 50-100 hours of use. You should also clean the air filter, check the spark plug, and always use fuel stabilizer if storing gasoline.

**Are expensive inverter generator worth it?**  
Yes, premium brands like Honda are worth the extra cost if you use your generator frequently or depend on it for survival. Their build quality and longevity often save you money over several years of use.

**What brands make the best inverter generator?**  
Honda is the premium leader, while Westinghouse and WEN offer the best balance of price, power, and modern features like remote start and dual-fuel capabilities.

**How long do inverter generator typically last?**  
A well-maintained inverter generator can last between 1,000 and 3,000 hours. Premium models like Honda are known to exceed these numbers significantly.

**Can I find good inverter generator on a budget?**  
Absolutely. WEN specializes in high-quality, budget-friendly inverters that consistently perform well in testing while offering essential safety and maintenance features.

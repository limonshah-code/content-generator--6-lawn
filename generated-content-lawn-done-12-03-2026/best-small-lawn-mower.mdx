## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Small Lawn Mower

### 1. Find Your Need
Before you buy, assess the size and layout of your yard. If you have many flower beds or narrow paths, a 13-inch or 16-inch deck is essential for maneuverability. Consider if you have steep hills; if so, a lightweight model or a self-propelled unit will save your back. Manual reel mowers are perfect for flat, tiny patches but will struggle on uneven or overgrown terrain.

### 2. Budget
Setting a realistic budget helps narrow down your choices quickly. Manual reel mowers typically range from $80 to $130, offering the lowest entry point. Corded electric models usually sit between $100 and $200. Battery-powered cordless mowers are the most popular but expensive, ranging from $250 for basic kits to over $500 for high-voltage, self-propelled systems.

### 3. Key Features
Look for features that make the job easier, such as single-lever height adjustment. This allows you to change the cutting height of all four wheels simultaneously rather than adjusting each one individually. Brushless motors are another key feature to look for in battery mowers, as they provide more torque and a longer tool life.

### 4. Quality & Durability
Check the materials used in the deck construction. Plastic decks are lightweight and rust-proof, which is great for small mowers, but they can crack if they hit a large rock. Steel decks are more traditional and durable but require more maintenance to prevent rust. Ensure the handle feels sturdy and the wheels have a decent tread for traction.

### 5. Brand Reputation
Buying from established brands like Greenworks, WORX, or BLACK+DECKER ensures you will have access to replacement parts and customer support. It also means you can often use the same batteries across multiple tools, which saves money as you expand your garden collection.

### 6. Portability/Size
One of the main reasons to buy a small mower is ease of storage. Look for models with handles that fold completely down or mowers designed for vertical storage. The weight is also critical if you need to carry the mower up stairs or lift it onto a shelf in your garage.

## Is it A Wise Decision to Buy small lawn mower?

Investing in a small lawn mower is an exceptionally wise decision for several reasons. First and foremost, it saves you a significant amount of money in the long run compared to hiring a professional service or maintaining a large, gas-powered machine. These mowers are specifically engineered for the needs of modern homeowners with limited outdoor space, focusing on efficiency rather than raw, unnecessary power.

When compared to alternatives like traditional gas mowers, small electric or manual models offer a much lower total cost of ownership. You won't have to deal with gasoline, oil changes, spark plugs, or expensive engine repairs. Furthermore, the environmental benefits are undeniable. By choosing an electric or manual mower, you are reducing your carbon footprint and eliminating noise pollution, which your neighbors will certainly appreciate. The long-term benefits include a healthier lawn thanks to cleaner cuts and a more organized garage due to the compact nature of these tools.

## What Is the Best Way to Choose small lawn mower?

Choosing the right small lawn mower starts with a clear understanding of your physical yard and your own comfort level. Follow these steps to find your perfect match:

1. **Measure Your Grass Area:** If your lawn is under 1,000 square feet, a manual reel or a small corded model is sufficient. For larger areas up to 1/2 acre, look into 40V or 60V cordless systems.
2. **Check Your Storage Space:** Measure the corner or wall where you plan to keep the mower. If space is tight, look for mowers with vertical storage features.
3. **Decide on Power Source:** If you have an outdoor outlet within 100 feet, a corded mower is the cheapest powered option. If you want total freedom, go cordless. If you want a workout and a silent cut, go manual.
4. **Evaluate Your Physical Strength:** If you find pushing heavy objects difficult, prioritize models under 35 pounds or look for self-propelled options.
5. **Research the Battery Ecosystem:** If you already own a 40V Greenworks drill, it makes sense to buy a Greenworks 40V mower so you can share batteries.

Avoid the common mistake of buying a mower that is too large for your yard just because it has more power. A larger mower is harder to turn and store, which will eventually make you dread the chore.

## FAQ

### What is the best small lawn mower for beginners?
The Greenworks 40V 16-inch model is excellent for beginners. It is lightweight, has a simple push-button start, and requires virtually no maintenance compared to a gas engine. It provides a familiar experience without the complexity of fuel ratios or pull-cords.

### How much should I spend on small lawn mower?
For a reliable powered model, expect to spend between $200 and $350. While you can find manual mowers for under $100, the extra investment in a battery-powered unit significantly reduces the physical effort required to keep your lawn tidy.

### What features are most important in small lawn mower?
Focus on the cutting width, height adjustment ease, and storage size. A 16-inch width is usually the sweet spot for small yards. A single-lever height adjustment is also a major convenience that you will appreciate every time you mow.

### How do I maintain my small lawn mower?
Electric and manual mowers are low maintenance. Keep the blade sharp and clean the underside of the deck after each use to prevent grass buildup. For battery models, store the batteries in a cool, dry place and don't leave them completely discharged for long periods.

### Are expensive small lawn mower worth it?
Yes, if they offer features like brushless motors or self-propulsion. A brushless motor lasts longer and is more efficient, while self-propulsion is worth every penny if you have a hilly yard or find pushing difficult.

### What brands make the best small lawn mower?
Greenworks, WORX, and BLACK+DECKER are top-tier brands for electric models. For manual reel mowers, the American Lawn Mower Company and LawnMaster are highly respected for their blade quality and durability.

### How long do small lawn mower typically last?
A well-maintained electric mower can easily last 5 to 10 years. Manual reel mowers can last even longer, often decades, as long as the blades are sharpened and the moving parts are kept lubricated.

### Can I find good small lawn mower on a budget?
Absolutely. Corded electric mowers and manual reel mowers offer great performance for under $150. They are perfect for those who want a great-looking lawn without a massive upfront investment.

## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Impact Driver

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you purchase, determine if you are looking for a 1/4-inch hex driver for screws or a 1/2-inch impact wrench for bolts. A hex driver is essential for building decks, cabinetry, and general construction. An impact wrench is better for automotive work. Also, check if you already own batteries for a specific brand, as buying a bare tool can save you significant money if you stay within one ecosystem like DEWALT or Milwaukee.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($100-$500 ranges)
Impact drivers range from $100 for basic kits to over $500 for professional high-torque sets. For occasional household use, a budget of $120 to $180 will get you a reliable brushless kit. Professionals should expect to spend $250 or more for higher RPMs, better ergonomics, and more durable internal components that can withstand daily abuse on a job site.

### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for the torque rating, usually measured in inch-pounds for drivers and foot-pounds for wrenches. Higher numbers mean more power. RPM (rotations per minute) and IPM (impacts per minute) are also vital; higher speeds allow you to work faster. Variable speed triggers and multiple speed modes are essential for maintaining control over different materials.

### 4. Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Choose tools with brushless motors, as they are more efficient, run cooler, and last significantly longer than brushed motors. Look for all-metal gear housings and high-quality plastics. Features like shock-absorbing battery connections help prevent internal damage from the vibrations inherent in impact tools.

### 5. Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Buying from established brands like DEWALT, Milwaukee, or CRAFTSMAN ensures you have access to warranties and a wide range of compatible tools. These brands have extensive service networks and their battery platforms are likely to remain available for many years, protecting your investment.

### 6. Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage

## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Walk Behind Lawn Mower Under 400

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
The first step is evaluating your terrain and grass type. If you have thick brush or weeds, a string mower like the Earthquake is essential. For standard manicured lawns, a reel mower provides a healthier cut but requires more regular maintenance of the grass height. Gas mowers are better for larger areas where manual effort might become exhausting.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($100-$400 ranges)
Under 400 dollars, you have two main paths. Manual reel mowers usually fall between 100 and 200 dollars, offering the best value for small yards. Gas-powered push mowers and specialized string mowers occupy the 250 to 400 dollar range. Higher prices in this bracket usually mean better engines, wider decks, or more durable blade materials.

### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for adjustable cutting heights with at least 3-5 settings. For gas mowers, check the engine displacement (cc) and torque. For reel mowers, the number of blades (5 is standard) and the width of the cutting path are the most critical specs. A grass catcher is a valuable bonus that saves time on cleanup.

### 4. Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Steel decks are generally superior to plastic ones for longevity and vibration dampening. Look for heat-treated or alloy steel blades that will stay sharp over several seasons. In gas models, a 4-cycle engine is preferred over 2-cycle because it is quieter and does not require mixing fuel and oil.

### 5. Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Buying from established names like Scotts, American Lawn Mower Company, or Earthquake ensures you have access to replacement parts and warranty service. These brands have decades of history in lawn care, providing more reliable engineering than generic, no-name imports.

### 6. Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage
Consider where you will store the mower. Many reel mowers have handles that fold down or can be hung on a wall. Gas mowers are heavier and require a dedicated floor space in a garage or shed. If you have tight gates or narrow paths, a 14 or 16-inch mower will be much easier to manage than a 22-inch model.

## Is it A Wise Decision to Buy walk behind lawn mower under 400?

Investing in a walk behind lawn mower under 400 is an exceptionally wise decision for the average homeowner. Modern engineering has progressed to the point where entry-level and mid-range mowers provide professional-looking results without the thousand-dollar price tag of riding mowers or high-end self-propelled units.

By choosing a mower in this category, you avoid the complexity and high maintenance costs of more expensive machinery. Manual reel mowers, for instance, have almost zero recurring costs and can last for a decade with just occasional blade sharpening. Even gas models in this price range are now built with high-torque OHV engines that are remarkably fuel-efficient and easy to start. Compared to hiring a professional lawn service, a 300 dollar mower pays for itself in just a few months. Furthermore, these mowers are generally lighter and easier to maneuver, making them accessible to a wider range of users while still delivering the power needed for a clean, healthy lawn.

## What Is the Best Way to Choose walk behind lawn mower under 400?

To choose the best model, start by measuring your lawn. If your yard is less than a quarter-acre and relatively flat, a manual reel mower is your best bet for health and silence. If you have more than a half-acre or hilly terrain, the power of a gas-driven motor like the Bilt Hard or Earthquake will save you from physical exhaustion.

Next, consider your physical comfort. Look for mowers with cushioned, ergonomic handles and adjustable heights. If you hate the smell of gasoline and the noise of engines, prioritize the Scotts or American Lawn Mower reel options. Avoid the mistake of buying more power than you need; a massive string mower is overkill for a small patch of fescue. Conversely, do not try to use a 14-inch manual mower on a field of thick weeds. Always check the warranty—a 5-year warranty is a strong indicator of a manufacturer's confidence in their product. Finally, read user reviews specifically regarding the ease of assembly and the reliability of the start mechanism for gas models.

## FAQ

**What is the best walk behind lawn mower under 400 for beginners?**
For beginners, the American Lawn Mower 16-Inch Reel Mower is ideal. It requires no engine maintenance, no fuel mixing, and is extremely easy to push and maneuver. It offers a simple entry into lawn care while providing a high-quality cut that keeps grass healthy.

**How much should I spend on walk behind lawn mower under 400?**
If you have a small, flat yard, spending 120 to 180 dollars on a quality reel mower is sufficient. For larger yards or thicker grass, expect to spend between 280 and 380 dollars for a reliable gas-powered or heavy-duty string mower.

**What features are most important in walk behind lawn mower under 400?**
The most important features are the cutting width, blade quality (look for alloy steel), and height adjustability. For gas models, a reliable 4-cycle engine and ball-bearing wheels are crucial for ease of use and longevity.

**How do I maintain my walk behind lawn mower under 400?**
For manual mowers, keep the blades clean and sharpen them every 1-2 seasons. For gas mowers, check the oil levels before each use, clean the air filter regularly, and use fresh fuel. Always clear your lawn of stones and large branches before mowing.

**Are expensive walk behind lawn mower under 400 worth it?**
Yes, moving toward the 300-400 dollar end of this budget usually nets you better engine displacement, wider cutting decks, and more durable materials like reinforced steel, which significantly extends the life of the machine.

**What brands make the best walk behind lawn mower under 400?**
Scotts and American Lawn Mower Company are leaders in manual reel mowers. Earthquake is a top choice for specialized string mowers, while Bilt Hard offers excellent value in the budget gas-powered category.

**How long do walk behind lawn mower under 400 typically last?**
A well-maintained manual reel mower can easily last 10 to 15 years. A gas-powered mower in this price range typically lasts 7 to 10 years if the engine is properly serviced and the deck is kept clean of debris.

**Can I find good walk behind lawn mower under 400 on a budget?**

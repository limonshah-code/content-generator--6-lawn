## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Lawn Mower Under 150

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before making a purchase, assess the size and terrain of your yard. If you have a small, flat patch of grass, a manual reel or corded electric mower is often the most cost-effective solution. For larger areas or yards with hills, a gas-powered self-propelled model or a high-voltage cordless mower will save you significant physical effort. Consider if you want to bag your clippings for a clean look or mulch them to provide natural fertilizer back into the soil.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($X-$Y ranges)
While our focus is under 150, prices can fluctuate based on seasonal sales and inclusions like extra batteries. Manual reel mowers typically sit in the $80 to $110 range, while entry-level gas and corded electric models fall between $120 and $150. If you are looking at cordless battery options, ensure the batteries are included in the price, as buying them separately can often double the total cost.

### Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for adjustable cutting heights with at least four positions to accommodate different seasons. The deck width is also critical; a 21-inch deck covers more ground than a 14-inch deck but is harder to maneuver in tight spaces. For electric models, check the amperage or voltage ratings to ensure the motor has enough torque to handle your specific grass type without stalling.

### Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
A steel deck is generally more durable and resistant to rocks or debris than a plastic deck, though it is heavier. Check for wheels with ball bearings, as these will roll much more smoothly and last longer over time. High-quality blades that can be sharpened easily will extend the life of your mower significantly and ensure a clean cut for years to come.

### Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Buying from established brands like Greenworks, PowerSmart, or American Lawn Mower Company ensures that you can find replacement parts and service centers if something goes wrong. These companies often offer better warranties, ranging from 2 to 4 years, which is vital for budget-friendly machines that might be pushed to their limits.

### Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage
If you have a small garage or a storage shed, look for mowers with foldable or collapsible handles. Lightweight models under 30 pounds can even be hung on wall hooks to save floor space. Consider how you will transport the mower; if you need to lift it into a vehicle or carry it up steps, a lightweight electric model is vastly superior to a heavy gas-powered unit.

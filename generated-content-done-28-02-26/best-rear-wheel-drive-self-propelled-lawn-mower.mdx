## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Rear Wheel Drive Self Propelled Lawn Mower

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before making a purchase, assess the size and terrain of your yard. Rear wheel drive mowers are ideal for hilly or uneven landscapes because the weight of the mower shifts to the back wheels as it climbs, providing better traction. If you already own a mower and just need repairs, ensure replacement parts like cables and wheels are compatible with your specific model number and year.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($X-$Y ranges)
Self-propelled gas mowers typically range from $300 to $600 depending on the engine brand and deck size. Entry-level models with generic engines are more affordable, while those with Briggs and Stratton or Honda engines command a premium. Replacement parts like cables are usually under $25, and high-quality wheel sets range from $30 to $60.

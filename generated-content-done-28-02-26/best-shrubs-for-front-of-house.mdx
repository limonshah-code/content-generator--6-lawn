## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Shrubs For Front Of House

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you make a purchase, identify exactly where the shrubs will be placed. If you have a wide porch, taller 4-foot trees like the Mavis Diary set can provide the necessary scale. For lining a narrow walkway, smaller ball-shaped topiaries or boxwood bundles might be more appropriate. Look for UV resistance if the shrubs will be in direct sunlight, as this prevents the greenery from turning blue or gray over time. Investing in artificial shrubs for the front of your house is an exceptionally wise decision for several reasons. First and foremost is the cost-effectiveness over time. Real shrubs require consistent watering, fertilizer, and professional pruning, not to mention the risk of them dying due to pests or weather changes. Artificial options provide a one-time investment that remains evergreen for years.

Compared to traditional gardening, these shrubs offer a stress-free alternative. You never have to worry about your curb appeal fading during a drought or freezing winter. They are particularly beneficial for people with limited mobility, busy professionals, or those who travel frequently and cannot tend to live plants. Additionally, for shaded areas where real plants refuse to grow, high-quality artificial topiaries can bring life to otherwise dead zones. The long-term aesthetic benefits and the boost to your home's market value make this a smart, practical choice for any homeowner.

## What Is the Best Way to Choose shrubs for front of house?

Choosing the right shrubs involves a few strategic steps to ensure the best look for your specific home.

1.  **Measure the Scale:** Use a measuring tape to see how much height your porch can handle. A tree that is too small looks lost, while one that is too large can block your door.
2.  **Match Your Style:** If your house is modern, go for the square pots and clean lines of cypress or cedar. For a traditional farmhouse look, boxwood balls and bundles are ideal.
3.  **Check the Sun Exposure:** Even if you buy artificial, check the UV rating. Areas with 8+ hours of sun need high-level UV protection to maintain their color.
4.  **Consider the Base:** If you live in a windy area, prioritize models with ground spikes or plan to buy heavy decorative planters to house the small plastic pots they come in.
5.  **Read Recent Reviews:** Look for photos from other buyers to see how the shrubs look in real-world lighting, as studio photos can sometimes be misleading.
6.  **Don't Forget Symmetry:** Most front-of-house designs look best with pairs. If you are framing a door, always buy a set of two for a balanced, professional appearance.

## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Lawn Mower Under 600

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you click buy, you must evaluate the size and terrain of your yard. If you have a small, flat lot, a 13-inch or 14-inch push mower is likely all you need to keep things tidy. However, for properties over a quarter-acre or those with hills, you should prioritize self-propelled models or high-voltage systems like 60V or 80V to ensure you do not run out of power before the job is done.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($300-$600 ranges)
In the sub-600 category, you will find a wide range of quality. Entry-level models around $300 are typically smaller and made of plastic, while the $500 to $600 range introduces features like self-propulsion, multi-battery kits, and steel decks. Decide if you want to save money upfront or invest slightly more for features that reduce physical effort.

### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for brushless motors as they are more efficient and last longer. Battery capacity (measured in Ah) determines your runtime, while voltage (V) determines the cutting power. Also, check the deck size; a 21-inch deck will cut your lawn faster than a 14-inch deck by covering more ground in each pass.

### 4. Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
While plastic decks are great for weight and rust resistance, steel decks offer better protection against debris impact. Check the wheel quality; large rear wheels provide better stability on uneven ground. Look for brands that offer at least a 2-to-4-year warranty on both the tool and the battery.

### 5. Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Stick with brands that have a proven track record in battery technology or engine manufacturing.

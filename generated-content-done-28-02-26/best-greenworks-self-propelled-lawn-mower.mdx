## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Greenworks Self Propelled Lawn Mower

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you choose a model, evaluate the size of your lawn and your existing tool collection. Greenworks offers 24V, 40V, 60V, and 80V platforms. If you already have 40V trimmers, sticking with a 40V mower allows you to share batteries.

## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Fungicide For Dollar Spot

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you purchase any fungicide, you must identify whether you are looking for a preventative or a curative solution. Many products, like those containing Propiconazole, offer both, but the application timing varies. Additionally, consider the grass type you have. Some fungicides are optimized for cool-season grasses like Kentucky Bluegrass, while others are universal. If you have a flower bed or ornamental shrubs near your lawn, choosing a multi-use product like Gravex 20 EW can save you time and money.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($20-$80 ranges)
Lawn care costs can vary wildly depending on the concentration and delivery method. 
- **$20-$30:** You will typically find ready-to-spray hose-end bottles or small granular bags. These are great for one-time issues on small lawns.
- **$40-$60:** This range usually includes professional-grade concentrates (16oz-32oz) that require manual mixing but offer multiple applications.
- **$70+:** This is the realm of bulk sizes (gallons) or high-potency systemic granules. While more expensive upfront, they offer the best value per square foot for large properties.

### Key Features - Most important technical specifications
The most critical specification is the active ingredient. In the world of fungicides, durability refers to how long the product stays effective after application. Rainproof capabilities are essential.
